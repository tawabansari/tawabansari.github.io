At-Talaq 65:1

ۖ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ

O Prophet, when you divorce women, divorce them for their waiting period and count the waiting period. Be mindful of The God, your Lord. Do not expel them from their homes, nor should they leave, unless they commit an obvious indecency. These are The God’s limits; whoever transgresses The God’s limits has wronged himself. You do not know; perhaps The God will bring about something new after that.
