Al-Ahqaf 46:15

ۖ ۖ ۚ ۖ

We enjoined upon the human being kindness to his parents. His mother carried him with hardship and gave birth to him with hardship; his carrying and weaning are thirty months. When he reaches his full strength and reaches forty years, he says: My Lord, inspire me to be grateful for Your favor which You bestowed upon me and my parents, and to do good that pleases You, and make my offspring righteous for me. I have turned to You, and I am among those who submit.
