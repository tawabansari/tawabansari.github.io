Yusuf 12:100

ۖ ۖ ۚ ۚ

He raised his parents upon the throne, and they fell in prostration to him. He said, “My father, this is the fulfillment of my earlier dream. My Lord has made it true. He was good to me when He brought me out of prison and brought you from the desert after Satan stirred discord between me and my brothers. My Lord is subtle in what He wills. He is the Knowing, the Wise.
