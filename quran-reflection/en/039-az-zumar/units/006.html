Az-Zumar 39:6

ۚ ۚ ۖ ۖ

He created you from a single soul, then made its mate from it, and sent down for you eight pairs of livestock. He creates you in your mothers’ bellies, creation after creation, in three darknesses. That is The God, your Lord. His is the dominion. There is no god except Him. How then are you turned away?
