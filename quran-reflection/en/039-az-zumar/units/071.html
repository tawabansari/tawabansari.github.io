Az-Zumar 39:71

ۖ ۚ

Those who disbelieved will be driven to Hell in groups, until, when they reach it, its gates are opened and its keepers say to them, “Did messengers from among yourselves not come to you, reciting your Lord’s signs to you and warning you of the meeting of this day of yours?” They will say, “Yes, but the word of punishment has come true against the disbelievers.”
