Az-Zumar 39:7

ۖ ۖ ۗ ۗ ۚ

If you disbelieve, The God has no need of you, and He does not approve disbelief for His servants. If you are grateful, He approves it for you. No bearer bears another’s burden. Then to your Lord is your return, and He will inform you of what you used to do. He knows what is within the breasts.
