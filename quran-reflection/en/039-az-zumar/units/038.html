Az-Zumar 39:38

ۚ ۚ ۖ

If you ask them who created the heavens and the earth, they will say, “The God.” Say, “Have you considered what you call upon apart from The God? If The God intends harm for me, can they remove His harm? Or if He intends mercy for me, can they withhold His mercy?” Say, “The God is sufficient for me. In Him those who trust place their trust.”
