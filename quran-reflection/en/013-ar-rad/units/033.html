Ar-Rad 13:33

ۗ ۚ ۗ ۗ

What of One who stands over every soul and what it has earned? Yet they assign partners to The God. Say, “Name them. Are you informing Him of something on earth He does not know, or merely of outward speech?” Rather, the scheming of those who disbelieve has been made fair to them, and they have been barred from the way. Whoever The God leads astray has no guide.
