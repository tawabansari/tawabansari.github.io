Ar-Rad 13:31

ۗ ۗ ۗ ۚ

If there were a Qur’an by which mountains were moved, the earth was split, or the dead were spoken to—rather, the whole affair belongs to The God. Have those who believe not realized that, had The God willed, He would have guided all people? Those who disbelieve will continue to be struck by a calamity for what they have wrought, or it will settle near their home, until the promise of The God comes. The God does not fail the appointment.
