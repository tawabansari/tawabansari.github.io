Ar-Rad 13:17

ۚ ۚ ۚ ۖ ۚ

He sent water down from the sky, and valleys flowed according to their measure. The torrent carried rising foam. From what they heat in the fire to make ornaments or utensils comes foam like it. Thus The God illustrates truth and falsehood. The foam passes away as refuse, while what benefits people remains in the earth. Thus The God sets forth examples.
