Ar-Rad 13:16

ۚ ۚ ۗ ۚ

Say, “Who is Lord of the heavens and the earth?” Say, “The God.” Say, “Have you taken protectors apart from Him who possess no benefit or harm even for themselves?” Say, “Are the blind and the seeing equal, or are darkness and light equal? Or have they assigned partners to The God who created as He creates, so that creation has become indistinguishable to them?” Say, “The God is Creator of all things. He is the One, the Overpowering.”
