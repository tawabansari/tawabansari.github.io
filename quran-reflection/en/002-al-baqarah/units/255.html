Al-Baqarah 2:255

ۚ ۚ ۗ ۚ ۖ ۚ ۖ ۚ

The God—there is no god except Him, the Living, the Sustainer. Neither slumber nor sleep overtakes Him. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on earth. Who can intercede with Him except by His permission? He knows what is before them and what is behind them. They encompass nothing of His knowledge except what He wills. His seat extends over the heavens and the earth, and preserving them does not weary Him. He is the High, the Great.
