Al-Baqarah 2:244

And fight in the way of The God, and know that The God is Hearing, Knowing.

Our Reflection

This verse comes immediately after the account of thousands of people who left their homes for fear of death. In the reflection on Al-Baqarah 2:243, we saw that migration to preserve life and freedom is an important principle in the Quran; yet the responsibility of individuals and societies does not end when they reach safety.

Now God says:

“وَقَاتِلُوا فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ”

“And fight in the way of The God.”

In the reflection on Al-Baqarah 2:190, we examined the meaning of “فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ.” God’s way is the way of guidance, justice, defending people’s lives and rights, freedom of belief and thought, helping the oppressed, and preventing aggression—not territorial conquest, imposing beliefs, seizing others’ property, or expanding power in the name of religion.

“قَاتِلُوا,” an imperative of Form III, commands fighting, combat, and confrontation.

“وَاعْلَمُوا أَنَّ اللَّهَ سَمِيعٌ عَلِيمٌ”

“And know that The God is Hearing, Knowing.”

“سَمِيعٌ” reminds us that God hears people’s words and requests, while “عَلِيمٌ” indicates that He also knows their intentions, motives, and deeds.

Merely claiming to fight “in God’s way” is therefore not enough. God knows who strives for justice and human freedom and who, under religious slogans, seeks power, revenge, or personal gain.
