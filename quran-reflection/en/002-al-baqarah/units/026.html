Al-Baqarah 2:26

Indeed, the One God is not restrained from setting forth a parable, even of a mosquito or what is above it. As for those who have faith, they know that it is the truth from their Lord. But as for those who cover truth, they say, “What did the One God intend by this as a parable?” Through it He leads many astray and through it He guides many; and He leads astray through it only the defiantly departing.

Our Reflection

This verse comes after several vivid parables: fire, darkness, storm, thunder, lightning, and now even the smallest living example. The Qur’an teaches that no image is too small when it carries truth.

Those who have faith do not measure a sign by outward size. They recognize that the Lord of creation can teach through the great and the small, through sky and rain, or through a tiny creature.

Those who cover truth respond differently. Their question is not a humble search for understanding; it is a dismissal. The same parable that opens recognition for one person becomes a cause of objection for another.

Guidance and misguidance are not arbitrary here. The parable reveals the reader’s posture. A heart seeking truth is guided through it; a heart already departing from bounds turns even the sign into an excuse for further departure.
