Al-Baqarah 2:24

But if you do not do it, and you will never do it, then guard yourselves against the Fire whose fuel is people and stones, prepared for the coverers of truth.

Our Reflection

This verse continues the challenge of the previous verse. If they cannot bring a surah like what has been sent down, the conclusion should not be pride. It should be taqwa: guard yourselves.

The phrase “and you will never do it” closes the door on the claim that revelation can be matched by human production. The failure reveals a boundary between what is sent down and what people construct apart from the One God.

The warning of the Fire is not abstract. Its fuel is described as people and stones. The verse moves the reader from rhetorical debate into moral seriousness.

The Fire is prepared for the coverers of truth. The issue is not honest seeking or humble questioning, but covering what has become clear.
