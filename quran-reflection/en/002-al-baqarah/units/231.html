Al-Baqarah 2:231

ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ

When you divorce women and they reach their term, retain them in fairness or release them in fairness. Do not retain them to harm them and transgress. Whoever does that has wronged himself. Do not take the signs of The God in mockery. Remember the favour of The God upon you and what He sent down to you of the Book and wisdom, admonishing you through it. Be mindful of The God, and know that The God knows all things.
