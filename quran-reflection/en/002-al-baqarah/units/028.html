Al-Baqarah 2:28

How do you cover truth regarding the One God, when you were lifeless, then He gave you life; then He causes you to die, then He gives you life again; then to Him you are returned?

Our Reflection

This verse asks a question rather than merely giving a command: how can you cover truth regarding the One God? The question is grounded in the most basic human reality: life was given, death will come, life will be given again, and return is to Him.

The verse does not begin with abstract argument. It begins with the human condition. Before this present life, the human being did not possess life by independent power. Life was received.

Then death interrupts any illusion of ownership. A person may gather power, identity, knowledge, and possessions, but cannot keep worldly life by personal command.

The same One who gave life first is able to give life again. Resurrection is placed within the same pattern of giving life after lifelessness.

The final movement is return. Human life is not a closed circle of birth and death with no moral end. It moves back to the One God, where covering truth, breaking covenant, and corrupting the earth cannot remain hidden.
