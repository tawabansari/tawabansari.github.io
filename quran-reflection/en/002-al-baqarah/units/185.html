Al-Baqarah 2:185

ۚ ۖ ۗ

The month of Ramadan, in which the Qur’an was sent down as guidance for people and clear proofs of guidance and discernment. Whoever among you witnesses the month, let him fast it; whoever is ill or on a journey, then a number of other days. The God desires ease for you, not hardship, so that you complete the number and magnify The God for guiding you, and so that you may give thanks.
