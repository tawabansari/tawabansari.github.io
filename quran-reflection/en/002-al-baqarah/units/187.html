Al-Baqarah 2:187

ۚ ۗ ۖ ۚ ۖ ۚ ۗ ۗ

Intimacy with your wives is permitted to you on the night of fasting. They are a garment for you and you are a garment for them. The God knew that you were betraying yourselves, so He turned to you and pardoned you. Now have relations with them and seek what The God has written for you. Eat and drink until the white thread of dawn becomes distinct to you from the black thread; then complete the fast until night. Do not have relations with them while you are in retreat in the mosques. These are the limits of The God, so do not approach them. Thus The God makes His signs clear to people, so that they may be mindful.
