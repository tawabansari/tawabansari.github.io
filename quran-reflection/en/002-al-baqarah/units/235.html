Al-Baqarah 2:235

ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ

There is no blame upon you for hinting at a proposal to women or concealing it within yourselves. The God knew that you would mention them. But do not make secret promises to them, except to speak honourable words. Do not resolve upon the marriage bond until the prescribed term is reached. Know that The God knows what is within you, so beware of Him; and know that The God is forgiving, forbearing.
