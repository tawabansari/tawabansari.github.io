Al-Baqarah 2:229

ۖ ۗ ۖ ۗ ۚ

Divorce is twice; then retention in fairness or release with kindness. It is not lawful for you to take back anything you have given them, unless both fear that they cannot uphold the limits of The God. If you fear that they cannot uphold the limits of The God, neither incurs blame for what she gives to ransom herself. These are the limits of The God, so do not transgress them. Whoever transgresses the limits of The God—those are the wrongdoers.
