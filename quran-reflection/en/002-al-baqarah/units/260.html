Al-Baqarah 2:260

ۖ ۖ ۖ ۚ

When Abraham said: My Lord, show me how You bring the dead to life. He said: Have you not believed? He said: Yes, but so that my heart may be reassured. He said: Take four birds and draw them toward yourself; then place a portion of them on every mountain, then call them: they will come to you swiftly. Know that The God is mighty, wise.
