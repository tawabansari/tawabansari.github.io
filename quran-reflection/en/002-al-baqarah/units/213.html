Al-Baqarah 2:213

ۚ ۖ ۗ

People were one community. Then The God sent prophets as bearers of good news and warnings, and sent down with them the Book in truth to judge between people concerning their differences. None differed over it except those given it, after clear proofs had come to them, through mutual transgression. Then The God guided those who believed, by His permission, to the truth over which they differed. The God guides whom He wills to a straight path.
