Al-Baqarah 2:283

ۖ ۗ ۚ ۗ

If you are on a journey and cannot find a scribe, then pledges taken into possession. If one of you entrusts another, let the one entrusted fulfil his trust and be mindful of The God, his Lord. Do not conceal testimony. Whoever conceals it has a sinful heart. The God knows what you do.
