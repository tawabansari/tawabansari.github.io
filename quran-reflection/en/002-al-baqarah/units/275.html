Al-Baqarah 2:275

ۚ ۗ ۚ ۖ ۖ

Those who consume riba do not rise except as one whom Satan has confounded through touch rises. That is because they say: Trade is only like riba. The God has permitted trade and forbidden riba. Whoever receives an admonition from his Lord and desists may keep what has passed, and his affair rests with The God. Whoever returns—those are the people of the Fire, remaining in it.
