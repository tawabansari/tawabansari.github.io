Al-Baqarah 2:282

ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۖ ۗ ۚ ۚ ۗ ۖ ۗ

O you who believe, when you contract a debt for a specified term, write it down. Let a scribe write between you in justice. No scribe should refuse to write as The God has taught him. Let him write, and let the one who owes the obligation dictate, being mindful of The God, his Lord, and diminishing nothing of it. If the one who owes the obligation is lacking in judgment, weak, or unable to dictate himself, let his guardian dictate in justice. Call two witnesses from your men. If there are not two men, then a man and two women from witnesses you accept, so that if one of the two errs, the other reminds her. Witnesses should not refuse when called. Do not tire of writing it down, small or large, with its term. That is more just with The God, firmer for testimony, and nearer to avoiding doubt, unless it is immediate trade you conduct among yourselves; then there is no blame upon you for not writing it. Take witnesses when you trade. Neither scribe nor witness shall be harmed. If you do that, it is disobedience on your part. Be mindful of The God. The God teaches you, and The God knows all things.
