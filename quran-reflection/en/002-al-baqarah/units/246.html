Al-Baqarah 2:246

ۖ ۖ ۖ ۗ

Have you not seen the leaders of the Children of Israel after Moses, when they said to a prophet of theirs: Appoint a king for us, so that we may fight in the way of The God? He said: Might you refrain from fighting if fighting were prescribed for you? They said: Why should we not fight in the way of The God when we have been driven from our homes and children? Yet when fighting was prescribed for them, they turned away, except a few. The God knows the wrongdoers.
