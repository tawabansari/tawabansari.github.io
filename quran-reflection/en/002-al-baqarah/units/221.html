Al-Baqarah 2:221

ۚ ۗ ۚ ۗ ۖ ۖ

Do not marry women who associate partners until they believe. A believing slave woman is better than a woman who associates partners, even if she pleases you. Do not give in marriage to men who associate partners until they believe. A believing slave is better than a man who associates partners, even if he pleases you. Those invite to the Fire; The God invites to the Garden and forgiveness by His permission, and makes His signs clear to people, so that they may remember.
