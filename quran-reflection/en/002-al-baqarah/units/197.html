Al-Baqarah 2:197

ۚ ۗ ۗ ۚ

Pilgrimage is in known months. Whoever undertakes pilgrimage in them, there is to be no intimacy, disobedience, or disputing during pilgrimage. Whatever good you do, The God knows it. Take provisions, for the best provision is mindfulness. Be mindful of Me, O people of understanding.
