Al-Baqarah 2:196

ۚ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۗ ۗ ۚ

Complete the pilgrimage and umrah for The God. If you are prevented, then whatever offering is attainable. Do not shave your heads until the offering reaches its destination. Whoever among you is ill or has an ailment of the head, then a ransom of fasting, charity, or sacrifice. When you are secure, whoever enjoys umrah until the pilgrimage, then whatever offering is attainable. Whoever cannot find one, then fasting three days during the pilgrimage and seven when you return: ten complete days. This is for one whose family does not reside near the Sacred Mosque. Be mindful of The God, and know that The God is severe in punishment.
