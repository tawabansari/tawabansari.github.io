Al-Baqarah 2:27

Those who break the covenant of the One God after its firm binding, and cut what the One God has commanded to be joined, and cause corruption in the earth: those are the losers.

Our Reflection

This verse explains who the “defiantly departing” are. They are not simply people who make a mistake. They break a covenant after it has been firmly bound, cut what should be joined, and spread corruption in the earth.

The covenant points to an entrusted bond with the One God. Human life is not ownerless. It is given with responsibility, recognition, and obligation toward truth.

Breaking the covenant does not remain private. The verse immediately moves to cutting relations that the One God commanded to be joined. A broken relation with truth produces broken relations in life: with people, kinship, justice, trust, mercy, and the created world.

The result is corruption in the earth. The loss begins as the loss of the very order that gives human life meaning, repair, and safety.
