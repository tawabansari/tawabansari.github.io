Al-Baqarah 2:253

ۘ ۖ ۚ ۗ ۚ

Those messengers: We favoured some over others. Among them were those to whom The God spoke, and He raised some in degrees. We gave Jesus, son of Mary, clear proofs and strengthened him with the Holy Spirit. Had The God willed, those after them would not have fought one another after clear proofs came to them. But they differed: some believed, and some disbelieved. Had The God willed, they would not have fought one another, but The God does what He intends.
