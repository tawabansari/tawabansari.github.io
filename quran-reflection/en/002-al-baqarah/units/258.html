Al-Baqarah 2:258

ۖ ۗ

Have you not seen the one who argued with Abraham about his Lord because The God had given him kingship? Abraham said: My Lord is the One who gives life and causes death. He said: I give life and cause death. Abraham said: Indeed, The God brings the sun from the east, so bring it from the west. The one who disbelieved was confounded. The God does not guide the wrongdoing people.
