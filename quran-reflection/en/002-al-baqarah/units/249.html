Al-Baqarah 2:249

ۚ ۚ ۚ ۗ

When Talut set out with the troops, he said: Indeed, The God will test you with a river. Whoever drinks from it is not of me, and whoever does not taste it is of me, except one who scoops a handful with his hand. They drank from it, except a few of them. When he and those who believed with him crossed it, they said: We have no strength today against Jalut and his troops. Those who expected to meet The God said: How many a small group has overcome a large group by the permission of The God! The God is with the patient.
