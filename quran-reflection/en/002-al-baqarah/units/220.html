Al-Baqarah 2:220

ۗ ۖ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ

On this world and the hereafter. They ask you about orphans. Say: Improving their affairs is best. If you mix with them, they are your brothers. The God knows the corrupter from the reformer. Had The God willed, He could have burdened you. Indeed, The God is mighty, wise.
