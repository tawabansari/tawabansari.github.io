Al-Baqarah 2:259

ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۚ

Or like the one who passed by a settlement fallen upon its roofs. He said: How will The God revive this after its death? The God caused him to die for a hundred years, then raised him. He said: How long have you stayed? He said: A day or part of a day. He said: Rather, you have stayed a hundred years. Look at your food and drink: they have not changed. Look at your donkey—and so that We may make you a sign for people. Look at the bones, how We raise them, then clothe them with flesh. When it became clear to him, he said: I know that The God has power over all things.
