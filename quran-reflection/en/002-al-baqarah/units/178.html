Al-Baqarah 2:178

ۖ ۚ ۗ ۗ

O you who believe, retribution concerning those killed is prescribed for you: the free for the free, the slave for the slave, and the female for the female. Whoever is pardoned anything by his brother, let there be pursuit in fairness and payment to him with kindness. This is a lightening and mercy from your Lord. Whoever transgresses after that will have a painful punishment.
