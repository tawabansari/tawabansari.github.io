Al-Baqarah 2:233

ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۗ ۗ ۗ

Mothers shall nurse their children for two complete years, for whoever wishes to complete the nursing. Their provision and clothing in fairness are the responsibility of the one for whom the child was born. No soul is charged beyond its capacity. No mother shall be harmed through her child, nor a father through his child; the heir bears the same responsibility. If both wish to wean by mutual consent and consultation, neither incurs blame. If you wish to have your children nursed, there is no blame upon you when you deliver what you give in fairness. Be mindful of The God, and know that The God sees what you do.
