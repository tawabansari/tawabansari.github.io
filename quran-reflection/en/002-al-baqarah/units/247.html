Al-Baqarah 2:247

ۚ ۚ ۖ ۚ

Their prophet said to them: Indeed, The God has appointed Talut as king for you. They said: How can he have kingship over us when we have more right to kingship than he, and he has not been given abundant wealth? He said: Indeed, The God has chosen him over you and increased him in knowledge and physique. The God gives His kingship to whom He wills. The God is all-encompassing, knowing.
