Al-Baqarah 2:1

Alif, Lam, Meem.

Our Reflection

This verse is from the disconnected letters, the ḥurūf muqaṭṭaʿāt; letters that appear at the beginning of some chapters of the Qur’an, while their definite and verifiable meaning is not clear to us. At the very opening, the Qur’an places the human being before an important truth: human understanding does not encompass everything.

So the appropriate response to these letters is to acknowledge the limits of our knowledge with humility before The God. A believer may reflect on these letters, but must not attribute a definite meaning to God when the Qur’an itself has not made that meaning clear.
