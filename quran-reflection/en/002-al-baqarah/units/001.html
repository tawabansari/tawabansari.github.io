Al-Baqarah 2:1

Alif, Lam, Meem.

Our Reflection

“الم” belongs to the Quran’s disconnected letters and is recited as “Alif, Lam, Meem.” These letters appear at the beginning of several surahs, but their definite and verifiable meaning is not clear to us.

Throughout history, various interpretations of the disconnected letters have been proposed, from divine names and symbolic signs to numerical calculations and connections with the themes of the surahs. Yet none of these possibilities may be attributed to God as the definite meaning of these letters without clear evidence.

We may research and reflect on these letters, but we must distinguish between what we know, what we consider possible, and what we do not know. Not knowing is no fault; the fault lies in inventing a meaning to conceal our lack of knowledge and imposing it on others in the name of divine truth.

The fact that their meaning is unclear to us does not mean that the Quran is incomplete or that its guidance depends on deciphering these letters. The Quran makes the path of guidance clear to human beings without their having to encompass the meaning of every part of it.

For a discussion of different views and the foundations of this understanding, see “The Disconnected Letters and the Limits of Human Knowledge before the Quran.”
