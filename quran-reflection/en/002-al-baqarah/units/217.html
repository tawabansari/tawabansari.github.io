Al-Baqarah 2:217

ۖ ۖ ۚ ۗ ۚ ۖ ۖ

They ask you about the sacred month, about fighting in it. Say: Fighting in it is grave; but barring from the way of The God, disbelief in Him, barring from the Sacred Mosque, and expelling its people from it are graver with The God. Trial is graver than killing. They will continue fighting you until they turn you back from your religion, if they can. Whoever among you turns back from his religion and dies as a disbeliever—their deeds have come to nothing in this world and the hereafter. They are the people of the Fire, remaining in it.
