Al-Baqarah 2:264

ۖ ۖ ۗ

O you who believe, do not invalidate your charities with reminders of generosity and hurt, like one who spends his wealth to be seen by people and does not believe in The God and the Last Day. His likeness is that of a smooth rock with soil upon it: a downpour strikes it and leaves it bare. They have no power over anything they have earned. The God does not guide the disbelieving people.
