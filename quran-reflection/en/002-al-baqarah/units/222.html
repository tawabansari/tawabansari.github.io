Al-Baqarah 2:222

ۖ ۖ ۖ ۚ

They ask you about menstruation. Say: It is a hurt. Keep apart from women during menstruation, and do not approach them until they become clean. When they have purified themselves, come to them from where The God has commanded you. Indeed, The God loves those who turn in repentance and those who purify themselves.
