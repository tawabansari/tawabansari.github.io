Al-Baqarah 2:228

ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۗ

Divorced women shall wait for three cycles. It is not lawful for them to conceal what The God has created in their wombs, if they believe in The God and the Last Day. Their husbands have a greater right to take them back during that time, if they desire reconciliation. Women have rights like their obligations, in fairness, and men have a degree over them. The God is mighty, wise.
