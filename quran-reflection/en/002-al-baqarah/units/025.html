Al-Baqarah 2:25

And give glad news to those who have faith and do works of repair: for them are gardens beneath which rivers flow. Whenever they are provided from them with fruit as provision, they say, “This is what we were provided before,” and they are given it in resemblance. And for them in them are purified companions, and they abide therein.

Our Reflection

After the warning of Fire, the Qur’an gives glad news. The contrast is deliberate: covering truth leads to burning consequence, while faith joined with works of repair opens into gardens, flowing rivers, renewed provision, purified companionship, and lasting life.

Faith is not separated from action. The verse says “those who have faith and do works of repair.” Truth believed inwardly must become repair, soundness, and restoration in life.

The gardens are not static. Rivers flow beneath them, and provision comes again and again. Each time, the people of the garden recognize continuity with what came before, yet what is given is also renewed.

The purified companions point to relationships cleansed from harms that corrupt worldly relations: betrayal, domination, impurity, insecurity, and decay.
