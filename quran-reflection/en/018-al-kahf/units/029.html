Al-Kahf 18:29

ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ

Say, “The truth is from your Lord. Let whoever wills believe, and let whoever wills disbelieve.” We have prepared for the wrongdoers a fire whose enclosure surrounds them. If they cry for relief, they will be given water like molten metal that scalds faces. What an evil drink and a dreadful resting place!
