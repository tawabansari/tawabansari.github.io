Al-Kahf 18:19

ۚ ۖ ۚ

In this way We raised them up so that they might question one another. One of them said, “How long have you remained?” They said, “We have remained a day or part of a day.” They said, “Your Lord knows best how long you have remained. Send one of you to the city with this silver of yours. Let him see which food is purest and bring you provision from it. Let him act discreetly and not make anyone aware of you.
