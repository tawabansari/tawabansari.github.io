Al-Kahf 18:22

ۖ ۚ ۗ

They will say, “Three, and their dog was the fourth,” and they say, “Five, and their dog was the sixth,” guessing at the unseen. They say, “Seven, and their dog was the eighth.” Say, “My Lord knows their number best. Only a few know them.” So dispute about them only with an evident argument, and do not ask any of them for an opinion about them.
