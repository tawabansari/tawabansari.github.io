Al-Kahf 18:21

ۖ ۖ ۚ

In this way We caused them to be discovered, so that people might know that the promise of The God is true and that there is no doubt about the Hour. When they disputed their affair among themselves, they said, “Build a structure over them. Their Lord knows them best.” Those who prevailed over their affair said, “We will certainly build a place of prostration over them.”
