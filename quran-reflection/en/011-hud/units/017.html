Hud 11:17

ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ

What of one who stands upon clear evidence from his Lord, followed by a witness from Him, and before it was the Book of Moses, a guide and a mercy? They believe in it. Whoever among the factions disbelieves in it, the Fire is his promised place. So have no doubt about it. It is the truth from your Lord, but most people do not believe.
