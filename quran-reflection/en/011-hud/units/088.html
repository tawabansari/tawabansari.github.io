Hud 11:88

ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ

He said, “My people, consider: if I stand upon clear evidence from my Lord and He has provided me with good provision from Himself. I do not wish to go against you by doing what I forbid you. I desire only reform as far as I can. My success is only through The God. On Him I have relied, and to Him I turn back.
