Al-Mujadilah 58:7

ۖ ۖ ۚ

Have you not seen that The God knows whatever is in the heavens and whatever is in the earth? There is no private conversation of three without Him being their fourth, nor of five without Him being their sixth, nor fewer or more than that without Him being with them wherever they are. Then, on the Day of Resurrection, He will inform them of what they did. The God knows all things.
