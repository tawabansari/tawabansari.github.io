Al-Mujadilah 58:22

ۚ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ

You will not find a people who believe in The God and the Last Day showing affection to those who oppose The God and His messenger, even if they are their fathers, sons, brothers, or kin. He has written faith in their hearts and strengthened them with a spirit from Him. He will admit them into gardens beneath which rivers flow, where they will remain forever. The God is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him. They are the party of The God. Indeed, the party of The God are the successful.
