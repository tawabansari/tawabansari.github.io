Al-Mujadilah 58:8

ۚ ۖ

Have you not seen those who were forbidden private conversations, then return to what they were forbidden and confer about sin, aggression, and disobedience to the messenger? When they come to you, they greet you with a greeting The God has not given you, and say within themselves, “Why does The God not punish us for what we say?” Hell is enough for them; they will burn in it. What an evil destination.
