Al-Anfal 8:42

ۚ ۙ ۗ

When you were on the nearer side, they were on the farther side, and the caravan was below you. Had you made an appointment with one another, you would have differed over the appointment. But this was so that The God might accomplish a matter that was to be done, so that whoever perished might perish on clear evidence and whoever lived might live on clear evidence. The God is Hearing, Knowing.
