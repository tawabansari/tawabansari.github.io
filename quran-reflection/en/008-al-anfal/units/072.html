Al-Anfal 8:72

ۚ ۚ ۗ

Those who believe, migrate, and strive with their wealth and their lives in the way of The God, and those who shelter and help them, are guardians of one another. As for those who believe but have not migrated, you have no guardianship of them until they migrate. If they seek your help in religion, you must help, except against a people with whom you have a treaty. The God sees what you do.
