Al-Hashr 59:2

ۚ ۖ ۖ ۚ

He is the one who expelled those of the People of the Book who disbelieved from their homes at the first gathering. You did not think they would leave, and they thought their fortresses would protect them against The God. Then The God came upon them from where they had not reckoned and cast terror into their hearts. They demolished their houses with their own hands and the hands of the believers. So take heed, you who have insight.
