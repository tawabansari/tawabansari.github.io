Al-An’am 6:71

ۗ ۖ

Say, “Shall we call upon, apart from The God, what neither benefits nor harms us, and be turned back on our heels after The God has guided us, like someone whom the devils have lured away on earth, bewildered, with companions calling him to guidance: ‘Come to us’?” Say, “The guidance of The God is the guidance, and we have been commanded to submit to the Lord of the worlds.”
