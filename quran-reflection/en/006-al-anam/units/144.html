Al-An’am 6:144

ۗ ۖ ۚ ۗ

And two of camels and two of cattle. Say, “Has He forbidden the two males, or the two females, or what the wombs of the two females contain? Or were you witnesses when The God instructed you in this?” Who is more unjust than one who invents a lie about The God to mislead people without knowledge? The God does not guide the unjust people.
