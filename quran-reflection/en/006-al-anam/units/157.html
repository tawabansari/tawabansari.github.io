Al-An’am 6:157

ۚ ۚ ۗ

Or lest you say, “If the Book had been sent down to us, we would have been better guided than they.” Clear evidence, guidance, and mercy have now come to you from your Lord. Who is more unjust than one who denies the signs of The God and turns away from them? We will repay those who turn away from Our signs with an evil punishment for their turning away.
