Al-An’am 6:70

ۚ ۗ ۖ

Leave those who have taken their religion as play and diversion and whom the worldly life has deceived. Remind by it, lest a soul be held captive for what it has earned, having neither guardian nor intercessor apart from The God. Whatever ransom it offers will not be accepted from it. Those are the ones held captive for what they earned; for them are a drink of scalding water and a painful punishment for their disbelief.
