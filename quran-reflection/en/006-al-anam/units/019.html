Al-An’am 6:19

ۖ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ

Say, “What is greatest in testimony?” Say, “The God is witness between me and you. This Qur’an has been revealed to me so that I may warn you by it, and whomever it reaches. Do you testify that there are other gods alongside The God?” Say, “I do not testify.” Say, “He is only One God, and I am free of what you associate as partners.”
