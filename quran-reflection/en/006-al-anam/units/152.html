Al-An’am 6:152

ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۚ

Do not approach the orphan’s property except in the best way, until he reaches maturity. Give full measure and weight with justice. We burden no soul beyond its capacity. When you speak, be just, even if it concerns a relative. Fulfill the covenant of The God. This He has enjoined upon you, so that you may take heed.
