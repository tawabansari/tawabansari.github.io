Al-An’am 6:128

ۖ ۚ ۗ

On the day He gathers them all: “Company of jinn, you have taken many among humans.” Their allies among humans will say, “Our Lord, some of us benefited from others, and we have reached the term You appointed for us.” He will say, “The Fire is your dwelling, remaining in it, except as The God wills.” Your Lord is Wise, Knowing.
