Al-An’am 6:145

ۚ

Say, “In what has been revealed to me, I find nothing forbidden to one who eats it except carrion, spilled blood, or the flesh of swine—for it is defilement—or a disobedient offering over which a name other than The God has been invoked. Whoever is compelled, neither desiring nor transgressing—your Lord is Forgiving, Merciful.”
