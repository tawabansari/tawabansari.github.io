Al-An’am 6:151

ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۚ

Say, “Come, I will recite what your Lord has forbidden you: associate nothing with Him; be good to parents; do not kill your children because of poverty—We provide for you and for them; do not approach indecencies, whether outward or hidden; and do not kill the soul that The God has made inviolable, except by right. This He has enjoined upon you, so that you may reason.”
