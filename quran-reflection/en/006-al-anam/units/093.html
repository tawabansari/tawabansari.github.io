Al-An’am 6:93

ۗ ۖ

Who is more unjust than one who invents a lie about The God, or says, “It has been revealed to me,” when nothing has been revealed to him, or one who says, “I will send down the like of what The God has sent down”? If you could see the unjust in the agonies of death, with the angels stretching out their hands: “Release your souls! Today you will be repaid with humiliating punishment for saying what was untrue about The God and for being arrogant toward His signs.”
