Al-An’am 6:91

ۗ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ

They did not esteem The God as He deserves when they said, “The God has sent down nothing to any human.” Say, “Who sent down the Book that Moses brought as light and guidance for people? You make it into sheets, displaying them and concealing much. You were taught what neither you nor your fathers knew.” Say, “The God.” Then leave them playing in their idle discourse.
