Al-Hadid 57:20

ۖ ۖ ۚ

Know that worldly life is play, amusement, adornment, boasting among yourselves, and rivalry in wealth and children. It is like rain whose growth delights the tillers; then it withers and you see it yellow, then it becomes broken straw. In the Hereafter there are severe punishment, and forgiveness and approval from The God. Worldly life is only the enjoyment of delusion.
