Al-Hadid 57:10

ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ

What is the matter with you that you do not spend in the way of The God, when the inheritance of the heavens and the earth belongs to Him? Those among you who spent and fought before the victory are not equal: they are greater in rank than those who spent and fought afterward. Yet The God has promised the finest to all. The God is aware of what you do.
