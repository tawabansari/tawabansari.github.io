Al-Hadid 57:27

ۖ ۖ

Then We followed in their footsteps with Our messengers, and followed with Jesus, son of Mary, and gave him the Gospel. We placed tenderness and mercy in the hearts of those who followed him. As for monasticism, they invented it; We had not prescribed it for them, except seeking the approval of The God. Yet they did not observe it as it deserved to be observed. We gave those among them who believed their reward, while many of them are defiantly disobedient.
