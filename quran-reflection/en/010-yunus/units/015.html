Yunus 10:15

ۙ ۚ ۖ ۖ

When Our clear signs are recited to them, those who do not hope to meet Us say, “Bring a Qur’an other than this, or change it.” Say, “It is not for me to change it of my own accord. I follow only what is revealed to me. If I disobey my Lord, I fear the punishment of a great day.”
