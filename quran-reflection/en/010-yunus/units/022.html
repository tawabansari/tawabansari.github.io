Yunus 10:22

ۖ ۙ

He is the One who enables you to travel on land and sea. When you are in ships, and they sail with them in a pleasant wind and they rejoice in it, a violent wind comes upon them and waves reach them from every side. They think they are surrounded and call upon The God, devoting religion sincerely to Him: “If You save us from this, we will surely be among the grateful.”
