Yunus 10:24

ۚ

The likeness of the worldly life is only like water We send down from the sky, with which the earth’s vegetation mingles, from which people and livestock eat. When the earth takes on its adornment and is beautified, and its people think they have power over it, Our command comes upon it by night or day, and We make it a harvest cut down, as though it had not flourished yesterday. Thus We set out the signs in detail for people who reflect.
