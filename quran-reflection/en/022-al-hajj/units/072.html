Al-Hajj 22:72

ۖ ۗ ۗ ۖ

When Our clear signs are recited to them, you recognize disapproval in the faces of those who disbelieved. They nearly attack those who recite Our signs to them. Say: shall I inform you of something worse than that? The Fire. The God has promised it to those who disbelieved. What a wretched destination!
