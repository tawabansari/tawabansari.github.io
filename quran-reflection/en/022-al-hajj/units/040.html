Al-Hajj 22:40

ۗ ۗ ۗ

those who were driven from their homes without right, only because they say: our Lord is The God. Had The God not repelled some people by means of others, monasteries, churches, synagogues, and mosques in which the name of The God is mentioned much would have been demolished. The God will surely help whoever helps Him. The God is strong, mighty.
