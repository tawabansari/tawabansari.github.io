Al-Hajj 22:18

ۖ ۗ ۚ ۩

Have you not seen that to The God prostrate whoever is in the heavens and whoever is on the earth, the sun, the moon, the stars, the mountains, the trees, the creatures, and many of the people? And upon many the punishment has become due. Whoever The God disgraces has none to honor him. The God does what He wills.
