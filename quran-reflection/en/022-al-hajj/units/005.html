Al-Hajj 22:5

ۚ ۖ ۚ

O people, if you are in doubt about the resurrection, We created you from dust, then from a drop, then from a clinging thing, then from a lump of flesh, formed and unformed, to make clear to you. We settle in the wombs what We will until an appointed term, then bring you out as infants, then that you may reach your full strength. Among you is one who is taken in death, and among you is one returned to the most feeble age, so that after knowledge he knows nothing. You see the earth lifeless; then when We send water down upon it, it stirs and swells and grows every delightful kind.
