Al-Hajj 22:78

ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۖ

Strive for The God with the striving due to Him. He chose you and placed no hardship upon you in religion—the creed of your father Abraham. He named you Muslims before and in this, so that the messenger may be a witness over you and you may be witnesses over people. Establish prayer, give zakat, and hold fast to The God. He is your protector. What an excellent protector and what an excellent helper!
