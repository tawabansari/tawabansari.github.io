At-Tawbah 9:74

ۚ ۚ ۖ ۚ

They swear by The God that they did not say it, but they did speak the word of disbelief and disbelieved after their submission. They intended what they did not attain. They resented only that The God and His Messenger had enriched them from His bounty. If they repent, it is better for them. If they turn away, The God will punish them with a painful punishment in this world and the Hereafter, and they will have no guardian or helper on earth.
