At-Tawbah 9:111

ۚ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ

The God has purchased from the believers their lives and their wealth in exchange for the Garden. They fight in the way of The God, killing and being killed—a true promise binding upon Him in the Torah, the Gospel, and the Qur’an. Who is more faithful to his covenant than The God? Rejoice in the bargain you have made. That is the great triumph.
