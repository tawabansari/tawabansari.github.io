At-Tawbah 9:120

ۚ ۚ

It is not for the people of Medina and the desert Arabs around them to stay behind the Messenger of The God or prefer their own lives to his. No thirst, weariness, or hunger afflicts them in the way of The God, nor do they tread any ground that angers the disbelievers, nor gain anything against an enemy, without a righteous deed being recorded for them because of it. The God does not let the reward of the doers of good be lost.
