At-Tawbah 9:37

ۖ ۚ ۗ

Postponement is only an increase in disbelief, by which those who disbelieve are led astray. They make it lawful one year and forbidden another year to match the number of what The God has made sacred, thus making lawful what The God has forbidden. The evil of their deeds has been made fair to them. The God does not guide the disbelieving people.
