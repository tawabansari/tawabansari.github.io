At-Tawbah 9:3

ۙ ۚ ۖ ۗ

An announcement from The God and His Messenger to the people on the day of the greater pilgrimage: The God is free of the associators, and so is His Messenger. If you repent, it is better for you. If you turn away, know that you cannot thwart The God. Give those who disbelieve news of a painful punishment.
