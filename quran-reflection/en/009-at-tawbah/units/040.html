At-Tawbah 9:40

ۖ ۗ ۗ

If you do not help him, The God already helped him when those who disbelieved expelled him, the second of two, when they were in the cave and he said to his companion, “Do not grieve; The God is with us.” The God sent down His tranquility upon him, strengthened him with troops you did not see, and made the word of those who disbelieved the lowest. The word of The God is the highest. The God is Mighty, Wise.
