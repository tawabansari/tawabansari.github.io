At-Tawbah 9:94

ۚ ۚ

They will make excuses to you when you return to them. Say, “Make no excuses; we will not believe you. The God has informed us of your news. The God and His Messenger will see your deeds. Then you will be returned to the Knower of the unseen and the seen, and He will inform you of what you used to do.”
