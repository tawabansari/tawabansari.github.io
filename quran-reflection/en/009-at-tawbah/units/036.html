At-Tawbah 9:36

ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ

The number of months with The God is twelve in the Book of The God, on the day He created the heavens and the earth. Four of them are sacred. That is the upright religion, so do not wrong yourselves during them. Fight the associators all together, just as they fight you all together. Know that The God is with the mindful.
