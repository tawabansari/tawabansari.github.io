Al-Ma’idah 5:41

ۛ ۛ ۖ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۖ

O messenger, do not be grieved by those who hasten into disbelief, from those who say with their mouths, “We believe,” while their hearts do not believe, and from those who became Jews: avid listeners to lies, listeners to other people who have not come to you. They distort words after their placement, saying, “If you are given this, take it; if you are not given it, beware.” Whoever The God wishes to put to trial, you have no power for him against The God. Those are the ones whose hearts The God has not wished to purify. Theirs is disgrace in this world and a great punishment in the Hereafter.
