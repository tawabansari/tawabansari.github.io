Al-Ma’idah 5:48

ۖ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۖ ۚ

We sent down the Book to you in truth, confirming what was before it of the Book and as a guardian over it. Judge between them by what The God has sent down, and do not follow their desires away from the truth that has come to you. For each of you We appointed a law and a way. Had The God willed, He would have made you one community, but to test you in what He has given you. So race toward good things. To The God is the return of you all, and He will inform you about what you used to differ over.
