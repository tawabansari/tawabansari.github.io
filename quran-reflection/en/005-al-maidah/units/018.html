Al-Ma’idah 5:18

ۚ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۖ

The Jews and Christians said, “We are The God’s sons and His beloved.” Say, “Why, then, does He punish you for your sins? Rather, you are human beings among those He created.” He forgives whom He wills and punishes whom He wills. To The God belongs the dominion of the heavens, the earth, and what is between them; and to Him is the destination.
