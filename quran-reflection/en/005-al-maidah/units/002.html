Al-Ma’idah 5:2

ۚ ۚ ۘ ۖ ۚ ۖ

O you who believe, do not violate The God’s sacred signs, the sacred month, the offerings, the garlands, or those heading to the Sacred House seeking bounty from their Lord and pleasure. When you leave the state of consecration, you may hunt. Do not let hatred of a people who barred you from the Sacred Mosque lead you to transgress. Cooperate in righteousness and mindfulness, and do not cooperate in sin and aggression. Be mindful of The God; indeed, The God is severe in punishment.
