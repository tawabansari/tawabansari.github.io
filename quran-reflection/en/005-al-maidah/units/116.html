Al-Ma’idah 5:116

ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ

When The God said, “Jesus son of Mary, did you say to people, ‘Take me and my mother as two gods besides The God’?” he said, “Exalted are You! It is not for me to say what I have no right to say. Had I said it, You would have known it. You know what is within myself, and I do not know what is within Yourself. Indeed, You are the Knower of the unseen.
