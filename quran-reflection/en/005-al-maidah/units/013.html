Al-Ma’idah 5:13

ۖ ۙ ۚ ۖ ۚ

For their breaking their covenant, We cursed them and made their hearts hard. They distort words from their places and forgot a portion of what they were reminded of. You will continue to discover treachery from them, except for a few of them. Pardon them and overlook; indeed, The God loves those who do good.
