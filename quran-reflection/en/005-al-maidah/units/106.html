Al-Ma’idah 5:106

ۚ ۙ

O you who believe, testimony among you when death approaches one of you, at the time of bequest, is by two just persons from among you, or two others from outside you if you are traveling through the land and the calamity of death befalls you. Detain the two after prayer, if you doubt, and let them swear by The God: “We will not exchange it for a price, even if it concerns a relative, and we will not conceal The God’s testimony; otherwise we would be among the sinful.”
