Al-Ma’idah 5:72

ۖ ۖ ۖ

Those who say, “The God is the Messiah, son of Mary,” have certainly disbelieved. The Messiah said, “Children of Israel, worship The God, my Lord and your Lord. Whoever associates partners with The God, The God has forbidden the Garden to him, and his refuge is the Fire. The wrongdoers have no helpers.”
