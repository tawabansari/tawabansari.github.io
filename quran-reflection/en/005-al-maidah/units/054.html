Al-Ma’idah 5:54

ۚ ۚ

O you who believe, whoever among you turns back from his religion, The God will bring a people He loves and who love Him, humble toward believers, firm toward disbelievers, striving in the way of The God and fearing no blame from any blamer. That is The God’s bounty, which He gives to whom He wills. The God is all-encompassing, knowing.
