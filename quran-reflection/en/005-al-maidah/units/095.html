Al-Ma’idah 5:95

ۚ ۗ ۚ ۗ

O you who believe, do not kill game while in a state of consecration. Whoever among you kills it intentionally owes recompense equivalent to what he killed, from livestock, judged by two just persons among you, as an offering reaching the Kaaba; or an expiation of feeding needy people, or its equivalent in fasting, so that he may taste the consequence of his act. The God has pardoned what is past. Whoever repeats it, The God will exact retribution from him. The God is mighty, possessor of retribution.
