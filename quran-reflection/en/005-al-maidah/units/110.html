Al-Ma’idah 5:110

ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ

When The God said, “Jesus son of Mary, remember My favor upon you and upon your mother, when I strengthened you with the Holy Spirit, speaking to people in the cradle and in maturity; when I taught you the Book, wisdom, the Torah, and the Gospel; when you formed from clay the shape of a bird by My permission, then breathed into it and it became a bird by My permission; when you healed the blind from birth and the leper by My permission; when you brought forth the dead by My permission; and when I restrained the Children of Israel from you when you brought them clear proofs, and those among them who disbelieved said, ‘This is nothing but manifest magic.’
