Al-Ma’idah 5:4

ۖ ۙ ۖ ۖ ۚ

They ask you what is lawful for them. Say, “Good things are lawful for you, and what you have trained of hunting animals, as trainers, teaching them from what The God has taught you. Eat from what they catch for you, and mention The God’s name over it. Be mindful of The God; indeed, The God is swift in reckoning.”
