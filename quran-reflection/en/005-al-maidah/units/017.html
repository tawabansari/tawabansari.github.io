Al-Ma’idah 5:17

ۚ ۗ ۚ ۚ

Those who say, “The God is the Messiah, son of Mary,” have certainly disbelieved. Say, “Who has any power against The God if He wishes to destroy the Messiah, son of Mary, his mother, and everyone on earth?” To The God belongs the dominion of the heavens, the earth, and what is between them. He creates what He wills, and The God is capable of all things.
