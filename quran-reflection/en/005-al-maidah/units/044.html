Al-Ma’idah 5:44

ۚ ۚ ۚ

We sent down the Torah, in which was guidance and light. The prophets who submitted judged by it for those who became Jews, as did the devoted teachers and scholars, by what they were entrusted to preserve of The God’s Book and to which they were witnesses. Do not fear people, but fear Me, and do not exchange My signs for a small price. Whoever does not judge by what The God has sent down, those are the disbelievers.
