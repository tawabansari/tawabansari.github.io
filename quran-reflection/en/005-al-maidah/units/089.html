Al-Ma’idah 5:89

ۖ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ

The God does not hold you accountable for idle speech in your oaths, but holds you accountable for the oaths you bind. Its expiation is feeding ten needy people from the average of what you feed your families, or clothing them, or freeing a slave. Whoever cannot find the means must fast three days. That is the expiation of your oaths when you swear. Guard your oaths. Thus The God makes His signs clear to you, so that you may be grateful.
