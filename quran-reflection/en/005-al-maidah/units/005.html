Al-Ma’idah 5:5

ۖ ۖ ۗ

Today good things are lawful for you. The food of those who were given the Book is lawful for you, and your food is lawful for them; and chaste women from the believers and chaste women from those given the Book before you, when you give them their dues, being chaste, neither fornicating nor taking secret lovers. Whoever rejects faith, his work has come to nothing, and in the Hereafter he is among the losers.
