Al-Ma’idah 5:12

ۖ ۖ ۚ

The God took the covenant of the Children of Israel, and We raised twelve leaders from among them. The God said, “I am with you. If you establish prayer, give zakat, believe in My messengers, support them, and lend The God a good loan, I will surely remove your misdeeds from you and admit you into gardens beneath which rivers flow. Whoever among you disbelieves after that has strayed from the sound way.”
