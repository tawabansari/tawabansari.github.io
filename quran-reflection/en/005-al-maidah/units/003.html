Al-Ma’idah 5:3

ۚ ۗ ۚ ۚ ۙ

Forbidden to you are carrion, blood, the flesh of swine, what has been invoked for other than The God, the strangled, the beaten to death, the fallen, the gored, and what a predator has eaten—except what you slaughter—and what is slaughtered on sacrificial stones, and seeking allotments by arrows. That is disobedience. Today those who disbelieve have despaired of your religion; do not fear them, but fear Me. Today I have perfected your religion for you, completed My favor upon you, and approved submission as your religion. Whoever is compelled by hunger, without inclining to sin—indeed, The God is forgiving, merciful.
