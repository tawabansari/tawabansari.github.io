Muhammad 47:15

ۖ ۖ ۖ

The description of the Garden promised to the mindful: in it are rivers of water that does not grow stale, rivers of milk whose taste has not changed, rivers of wine delightful to drinkers, and rivers of purified honey. They have in it every kind of fruit and forgiveness from their Lord. Is this like those who remain in the Fire and are given scalding water to drink that tears their intestines?
