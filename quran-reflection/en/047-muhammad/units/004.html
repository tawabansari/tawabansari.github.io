Muhammad 47:4

ۚ ۗ

When you meet those who disbelieve, strike the necks; then, when you have thoroughly subdued them, bind firmly. Thereafter, either release by grace or ransom, until war lays down its burdens. Thus it is. Had The God willed, He would have taken retribution from them, but it is to test some of you through others. Those killed in the way of The God—He will never let their deeds be lost.
