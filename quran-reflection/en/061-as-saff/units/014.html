As-Saff 61:14

ۖ ۖ ۖ

O you who believe, be helpers of The God, as Jesus, son of Mary, said to the disciples, “Who are my helpers toward The God?” The disciples said, “We are helpers of The God.” Then a group of the Children of Israel believed and a group disbelieved. We supported those who believed against their enemy, and they became victorious.
