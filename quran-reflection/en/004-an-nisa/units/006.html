An-Nisa 4:6

ۖ ۚ ۖ ۚ ۚ

Test the orphans until they reach marriageable age. If you discern sound judgment in them, hand over their wealth to them. Do not consume it extravagantly and hastily before they grow up. Whoever is wealthy should refrain, and whoever is poor may consume in a fitting manner. When you hand over their wealth to them, call witnesses over them. The God is sufficient as a reckoner.
