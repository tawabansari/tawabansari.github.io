An-Nisa 4:141

ۚ ۗ

Those who wait to see what happens to you: if you have a victory from The God, they say, “Were we not with you?” If the disbelievers have a share, they say, “Did we not have the upper hand over you and protect you from the believers?” The God will judge between you on the Day of Resurrection. The God will never grant the disbelievers a way over the believers.
