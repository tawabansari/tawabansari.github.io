An-Nisa 4:171

ۚ ۖ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۖ ۘ ۗ

O People of the Book, do not go to excess in your religion, and say nothing about The God except the truth. The Messiah, Jesus son of Mary, is only The God’s messenger and His word which He cast to Mary, and a spirit from Him. Believe in The God and His messengers, and do not say, “Three.” Desist; it is better for you. The God is only one God. Exalted is He above having a child. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is in the earth. The God is sufficient as a trustee.
