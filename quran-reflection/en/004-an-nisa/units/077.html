An-Nisa 4:77

ۚ ۗ

Have you not seen those who were told, “Restrain your hands, establish prayer, and give zakat”? When fighting was prescribed for them, a group of them feared people as they feared The God, or with greater fear. They said, “Our Lord, why have You prescribed fighting for us? Why did You not delay us until a near term?” Say, “The enjoyment of this world is little, and the Hereafter is better for whoever is mindful; you will not be wronged by so much as a thread.”
