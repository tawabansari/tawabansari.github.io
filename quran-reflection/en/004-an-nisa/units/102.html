An-Nisa 4:102

ۗ ۚ ۖ ۗ

When you are among them and establish prayer for them, let a group of them stand with you and take their weapons. When they have prostrated, let them be behind you, and let another group who have not prayed come and pray with you, taking their precautions and weapons. Those who disbelieve wish you would neglect your weapons and belongings so they could attack you in a single assault. There is no blame upon you for laying down your weapons if you are troubled by rain or are ill, but take your precautions. Indeed, The God has prepared a humiliating punishment for the disbelievers.
