An-Nisa 4:11

ۖ ۚ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۗ ۚ ۗ

The God instructs you concerning your children: for the male is the equivalent of the share of two females. If they are females numbering more than two, theirs is two-thirds of what he leaves; if there is one, hers is half. For his parents, each of the two has one-sixth of what he leaves if he has a child. If he has no child and his parents inherit from him, his mother has one-third. If he has siblings, his mother has one-sixth, after any bequest he makes or debt. Your fathers and your sons—you do not know which of them are nearer to you in benefit. This is an obligation from The God. Indeed, The God is knowing, wise.
