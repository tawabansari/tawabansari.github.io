An-Nisa 4:94

ۚ ۚ

O you who believe, when you go forth in the way of The God, ascertain matters. Do not say to one who offers you peace, “You are not a believer,” seeking the goods of worldly life; with The God are abundant gains. You were like that before, then The God favored you. So ascertain matters. Indeed, The God is aware of what you do.
