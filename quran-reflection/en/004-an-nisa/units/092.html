An-Nisa 4:92

ۚ ۚ ۖ ۖ ۗ

It is not for a believer to kill a believer except by mistake. Whoever kills a believer by mistake must free a believing slave and deliver compensation to his family, unless they waive it as charity. If he belonged to a people hostile to you and was a believer, then the freeing of a believing slave. If he belonged to a people with whom you have a treaty, then compensation delivered to his family and the freeing of a believing slave. Whoever cannot find the means must fast two consecutive months, as repentance from The God. The God is knowing, wise.
