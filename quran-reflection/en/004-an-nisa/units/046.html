An-Nisa 4:46

ۚ

Among those who became Jews are some who distort words from their places and say, “We hear and disobey,” “Hear, may you not be made to hear,” and “Attend to us,” twisting their tongues and attacking the religion. Had they said, “We hear and obey,” “Hear,” and “Look toward us,” it would have been better and more upright for them. But The God has cursed them for their disbelief, so they believe but little.
