An-Nisa 4:24

ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ

And married women, except what your right hands possess. This is The God’s decree upon you. Lawful to you is what lies beyond these, that you seek with your wealth, being chaste and not fornicating. For what you enjoy from them, give them their dues as an obligation. There is no blame upon you in what you mutually agree upon after the obligation. Indeed, The God is knowing, wise.
