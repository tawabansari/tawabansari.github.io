An-Nisa 4:95

ۚ ۚ ۚ

Those believers who remain seated, other than those with disabilities, are not equal to those who strive in the way of The God with their wealth and lives. The God has favored those who strive with their wealth and lives above those who remain seated by a degree. To each The God has promised the best. The God has favored those who strive above those who remain seated with a great reward.
