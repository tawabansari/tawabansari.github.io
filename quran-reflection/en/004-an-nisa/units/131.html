An-Nisa 4:131

ۗ ۚ ۚ

To The God belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is in the earth. We instructed those who were given the Book before you, and you, to be mindful of The God. If you disbelieve, then to The God belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is in the earth. The God is self-sufficient, praiseworthy.
