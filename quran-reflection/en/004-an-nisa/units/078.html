An-Nisa 4:78

ۗ ۖ ۚ ۖ

Wherever you are, death will overtake you, even if you are in fortified towers. If good befalls them, they say, “This is from The God”; if evil befalls them, they say, “This is from you.” Say, “All is from The God.” What is wrong with these people that they scarcely understand a statement?
