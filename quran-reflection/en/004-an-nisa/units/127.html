An-Nisa 4:127

ۖ ۚ

They ask you for a ruling concerning women. Say, “The God gives you a ruling concerning them, and what is recited to you in the Book concerning orphaned women to whom you do not give what is prescribed for them and whom you wish to marry, and the oppressed children, and that you uphold equity for orphans.” Whatever good you do, indeed, The God knows it.
