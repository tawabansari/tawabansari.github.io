An-Nisa 4:176

ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۗ ۗ

They ask you for a ruling. Say, “The God gives you a ruling concerning kalalah: if a man dies leaving no child but a sister, hers is half of what he leaves. He inherits from her if she has no child. If there are two sisters, theirs is two-thirds of what he leaves. If there are siblings, men and women, the male has the equivalent of the share of two females.” The God makes clear to you lest you go astray. The God knows all things.
