An-Nisa 4:135

ۚ ۖ ۚ

O you who believe, be upholders of equity, witnesses for The God, even against yourselves, parents, or close relatives. Whether one is rich or poor, The God has a greater claim over both. Do not follow desire, lest you deviate from justice. If you distort or turn away, indeed, The God is aware of what you do.
