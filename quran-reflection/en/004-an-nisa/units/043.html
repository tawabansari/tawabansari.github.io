An-Nisa 4:43

ۚ ۗ

O you who believe, do not approach prayer while intoxicated until you know what you are saying, nor in a state of major impurity, except when passing through, until you wash. If you are ill or on a journey, or one of you comes from relieving himself, or you have touched women and find no water, then seek clean earth and wipe your faces and hands. Indeed, The God is pardoning, forgiving.
