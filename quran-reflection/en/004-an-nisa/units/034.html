An-Nisa 4:34

ۚ ۚ ۖ ۗ

Men are maintainers of women by what The God has favored some of them over others and by what they have spent of their wealth. Thus righteous women are devout, guarding the unseen by what The God has guarded. Those whose defiance you fear, admonish them, abandon them in beds, and strike them. If they obey you, seek no way against them. Indeed, The God is exalted, great.
