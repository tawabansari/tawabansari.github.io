An-Nisa 4:12

ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۗ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۗ

Yours is half of what your wives leave if they have no child. If they have a child, yours is one-quarter of what they leave, after any bequest they make or debt. Theirs is one-quarter of what you leave if you have no child. If you have a child, theirs is one-eighth of what you leave, after any bequest you make or debt. If a man or woman being inherited from is kalalah, and has a brother or sister, each of the two has one-sixth. If they are more than that, they share one-third, after any bequest made or debt, without causing harm: an instruction from The God. The God is knowing, forbearing.
