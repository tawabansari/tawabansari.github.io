An-Nisa 4:90

ۚ ۚ

Except those who join a people with whom you have a treaty, or come to you with hearts constrained against fighting you or fighting their own people. Had The God willed, He could have given them power over you, and they would have fought you. If they withdraw from you, do not fight you, and offer you peace, The God has made no way for you against them.
