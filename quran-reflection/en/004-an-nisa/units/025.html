An-Nisa 4:25

ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۗ

Whoever among you lacks the means to marry free believing women, then from your believing girls whom your right hands possess. The God knows your faith best; you are of one another. Marry them with their families’ permission and give them their dues in a fitting manner, being chaste, neither fornicating nor taking secret lovers. Once they are married, if they commit indecency, they incur half the punishment of free women. This is for whoever among you fears hardship; but to be patient is better for you. The God is forgiving, merciful.
