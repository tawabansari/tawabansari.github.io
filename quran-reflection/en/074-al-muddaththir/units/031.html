Al-Muddaththir 74:31

ۙ ۙ ۙ ۚ ۚ ۚ

We appointed only angels as the keepers of the Fire, and made their number only a test for those who disbelieve, so that those given the Book may become certain and those who believe may increase in faith, and those given the Book and the believers may not doubt, and so that those with sickness in their hearts and the disbelievers may say, “What does The God intend by this example?” Thus The God leads astray whom He wills and guides whom He wills. None knows your Lord’s forces except Him. This is only a reminder for humankind.
