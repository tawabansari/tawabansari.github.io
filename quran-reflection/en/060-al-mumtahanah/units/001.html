Al-Mumtahanah 60:1

ۙ ۚ ۚ

O you who believe, do not take My enemy and your enemy as allies, offering them affection when they have denied the truth that has come to you and are expelling the messenger and you because you believe in The God, your Lord—if you have gone out to strive in My way and seek My approval. You secretly offer them affection, while I know best what you conceal and what you reveal. Whoever among you does this has strayed from the straight way.
