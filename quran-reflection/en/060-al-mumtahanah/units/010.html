Al-Mumtahanah 60:10

ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۖ ۚ

O you who believe, when believing women come to you as emigrants, examine them; The God knows their faith best. If you find them to be believers, do not return them to the disbelievers. These women are not lawful for those men, nor are those men lawful for them. Give the men what they have spent. There is no blame on you for marrying the women when you give them their bridal dues. Do not retain marital bonds with disbelieving women. Ask for what you have spent, and let them ask for what they have spent. This is the judgment of The God; He judges between you. The God is Knowing and Wise.
