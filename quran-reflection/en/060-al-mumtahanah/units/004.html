Al-Mumtahanah 60:4

ۖ

You have a good example in Abraham and those with him, when they said to their people, “We are free of you and whatever you worship besides The God. We reject you, and enmity and hatred have appeared between us and you forever, until you believe in The God alone”—except for Abraham’s words to his father, “I will surely ask forgiveness for you, though I have no power for you against The God.” “Our Lord, in You we trust, to You we turn, and to You is the final return.
