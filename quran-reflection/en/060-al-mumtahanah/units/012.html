Al-Mumtahanah 60:12

ۙ ۖ

O Prophet, when believing women come to you pledging that they will associate nothing with The God, will not steal, commit adultery, or kill their children, will not bring a slander they fabricate between their hands and feet, and will not disobey you in what is right, accept their pledge and ask The God to forgive them. The God is Forgiving and Merciful.
