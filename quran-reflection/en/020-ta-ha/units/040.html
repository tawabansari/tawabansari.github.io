Ta-Ha 20:40

ۖ ۚ ۚ

When your sister walked along and said, ‘Shall I direct you to someone who will care for him?’ We returned you to your mother so that she would be comforted and not grieve. You killed a person, and We delivered you from distress and tested you with trials. You remained for years among the people of Midian, then came at the appointed time, Moses.
