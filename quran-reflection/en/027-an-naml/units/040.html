An-Naml 27:40

ۚ ۖ ۖ

One who had knowledge from the Book said: I will bring it to you before your glance returns to you. When he saw it settled beside him, he said: This is from my Lord’s favor, to test me whether I give thanks or am ungrateful. Whoever gives thanks does so for himself; and whoever is ungrateful, my Lord is self-sufficient and generous.
