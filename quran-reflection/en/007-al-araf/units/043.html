Al-A’raf 7:43

ۖ ۖ ۖ

We will remove whatever rancor is in their breasts; rivers flow beneath them. They will say, “Praise belongs to The God, who guided us to this. We would not have been guided had The God not guided us. Our Lord’s messengers came with the truth.” They will be called, “This is the Garden you have been made to inherit for what you used to do.”
