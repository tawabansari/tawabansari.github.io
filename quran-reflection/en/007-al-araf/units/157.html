Al-A’raf 7:157

ۚ ۙ

Those who follow the Messenger, the ummī Prophet, whom they find written with them in the Torah and the Gospel. He commands them to what is right, forbids them what is wrong, makes good things lawful for them and impure things unlawful, and removes their burden and the shackles that were upon them. Those who believe in him, support him, help him, and follow the light sent down with him are the successful.
