Al-A’raf 7:73

ۗ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ

And to Thamud, their brother Salih. He said, “My people, worship The God; you have no god other than Him. Clear evidence has come to you from your Lord. This she-camel of The God is a sign for you; leave her to eat on the earth of The God, and do not touch her with harm, lest a painful punishment seize you.”
