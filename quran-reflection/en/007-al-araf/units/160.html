Al-A’raf 7:160

ۚ ۖ ۖ ۚ ۖ ۚ

We divided them into twelve tribes, communities. We revealed to Moses, when his people asked him for water, “Strike the rock with your staff.” Twelve springs gushed from it; each group knew its drinking place. We shaded them with clouds and sent down manna and quails to them: “Eat of the good things We have provided you.” They did not wrong Us, but they were wronging themselves.
