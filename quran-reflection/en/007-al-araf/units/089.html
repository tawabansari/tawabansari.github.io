Al-A’raf 7:89

ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ

We would have invented a lie about The God if we returned to your way after The God saved us from it. It is not for us to return to it unless The God, our Lord, wills. Our Lord encompasses everything in knowledge. In The God we trust. Our Lord, decide between us and our people in truth; You are the best of deciders.
