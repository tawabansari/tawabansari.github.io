Al-A’raf 7:176

ۚ ۚ ۚ

Had We willed, We would have raised him through them, but he clung to the earth and followed his desire. His likeness is that of a dog: if you drive it away, it pants, and if you leave it, it pants. That is the likeness of the people who deny Our signs. Relate the account, so that they may reflect.
