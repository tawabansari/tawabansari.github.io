Al-A’raf 7:169

ۚ ۗ ۗ

Successors came after them who inherited the Book, taking the goods of this lower life and saying, “We will be forgiven.” If similar goods came to them, they would take them too. Was the covenant of the Book not taken from them, that they say nothing about The God except the truth? They have studied what is in it. The abode of the Hereafter is better for those who are mindful. Will you not reason?
