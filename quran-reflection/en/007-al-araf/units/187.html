Al-A’raf 7:187

ۖ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۗ ۖ

They ask you about the Hour: when will it arrive? Say, “Knowledge of it is only with my Lord. None but Him will reveal it at its time. It weighs heavily in the heavens and the earth. It will come upon you only suddenly.” They ask you as though you were fully acquainted with it. Say, “Knowledge of it is only with The God, but most people do not know.”
