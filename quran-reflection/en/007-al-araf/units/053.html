Al-A’raf 7:53

ۚ ۚ

Are they waiting for anything but its fulfillment? On the day its fulfillment comes, those who forgot it before will say, “Our Lord’s messengers came with the truth. Do we have intercessors to intercede for us, or can we be sent back to act differently from how we used to act?” They have lost themselves, and what they used to invent has vanished from them.
