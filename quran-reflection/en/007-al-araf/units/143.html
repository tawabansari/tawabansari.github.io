Al-A’raf 7:143

ۚ ۚ ۚ

When Moses came at Our appointed time and his Lord spoke to him, he said, “My Lord, show me, that I may look at You.” He said, “You will not see Me, but look at the mountain. If it remains in its place, you will see Me.” When his Lord manifested Himself to the mountain, He made it crumble, and Moses fell unconscious. When he recovered, he said, “Glory be to You! I turn to You in repentance, and I am the first of the believers.”
