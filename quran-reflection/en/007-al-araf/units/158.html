Al-A’raf 7:158

ۖ ۖ

Say, “People, I am the messenger of The God to you all, the One to whom belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth. There is no god except Him; He gives life and causes death. Believe in The God and His Messenger, the ummī Prophet who believes in The God and His words, and follow him so that you may be guided.”
