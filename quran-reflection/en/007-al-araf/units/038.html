Al-A’raf 7:38

ۖ ۖ ۖ

He will say, “Enter the Fire among communities of jinn and humans that passed before you.” Whenever a community enters, it curses its sister community. When they have all joined one another in it, the last of them will say about the first, “Our Lord, these led us astray; give them a double punishment of the Fire.” He will say, “For each is double, but you do not know.”
