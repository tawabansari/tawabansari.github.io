Al-A’raf 7:37

ۚ ۖ ۖ

Who is more unjust than one who invents a lie about The God or denies His signs? Their portion from the Book will reach them, until Our messengers come to take them and say, “Where are those you called upon apart from The God?” They will say, “They have vanished from us,” and testify against themselves that they were disbelievers.
