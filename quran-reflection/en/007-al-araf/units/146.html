Al-A’raf 7:146

ۚ

I will turn away from My signs those who are unjustly arrogant on the earth. If they see every sign, they will not believe in it; if they see the way of sound guidance, they will not take it as a way, but if they see the way of error, they will take it as a way. That is because they denied Our signs and were heedless of them.
