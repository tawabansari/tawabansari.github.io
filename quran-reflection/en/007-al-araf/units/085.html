Al-A’raf 7:85

ۗ ۖ ۖ ۚ

And to Midian, their brother Shuayb. He said, “My people, worship The God; you have no god other than Him. Clear evidence has come to you from your Lord. Give full measure and weight, do not diminish people’s goods, and do not cause corruption on the earth after it has been set right. That is better for you, if you are believers.”
