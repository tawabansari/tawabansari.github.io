Al-A’raf 7:150

ۖ ۖ ۚ

When Moses returned to his people, angry and grieved, he said, “Evil is how you succeeded me after I left. Did you seek to hasten your Lord’s command?” He threw down the tablets and seized his brother by the head, pulling him toward himself. He said, “Son of my mother, the people overpowered me and nearly killed me. Do not make the enemies rejoice over me, and do not place me with the unjust people.”
