Ibrahim 14:9

ۛ ۛ ۚ

Has the news not reached you of those before you—the people of Noah, Ad, Thamud, and those after them? None knows them except The God. Their messengers came to them with clear proofs, but they put their hands back into their mouths and said, “We disbelieve in what you have been sent with, and we are in disturbing doubt about what you call us to.”
