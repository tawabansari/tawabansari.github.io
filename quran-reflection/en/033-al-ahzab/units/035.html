Al-Ahzab 33:35

Indeed, men who submit and women who submit, believing men and believing women, devoutly obedient men and devoutly obedient women, truthful men and truthful women, patient men and patient women, humble men and humble women, charitable men and charitable women, fasting men and fasting women, men who guard their private parts and women who guard them, and men who remember The God much and women who remember Him: The God has prepared for them forgiveness and a great reward.
