Al-Ahzab 33:37

ۖ ۚ

And when you said to the one whom The God had favored and whom you had favored: Keep your wife with you and be mindful of The God, while you concealed within yourself what The God was to disclose, and feared people, though The God had more right that you should fear Him. Then when Zayd had fulfilled his need of her, We married her to you so that there would be no constraint upon believers concerning the wives of their adopted sons when they have fulfilled their need of them. And the command of The God is to be accomplished.
