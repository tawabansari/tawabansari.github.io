Al-Ahzab 33:53

ۚ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ ۚ

O you who believe, do not enter the Prophet’s homes unless you are permitted for a meal, without waiting for its preparation. But when you are invited, enter; and when you have eaten, disperse, without lingering for conversation. Indeed, that was hurting the Prophet, and he was shy of you; but The God is not shy of the truth. And when you ask them for an item, ask them from behind a screen. That is purer for your hearts and their hearts. It is not for you to hurt the messenger of The God, nor ever to marry his wives after him. Indeed, that is grave with The God.
