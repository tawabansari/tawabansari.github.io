Al-Ahzab 33:51

ۖ ۚ ۚ ۚ

You may defer whom you will of them and receive whom you will with you. And there is no blame upon you if you seek any of those you had set aside. That is more likely for their eyes to be comforted, for them not to grieve, and for all of them to be content with what you have given them. And The God knows what is in your hearts. And The God is Knowing, Forbearing.
