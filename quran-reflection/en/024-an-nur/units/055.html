An-Nur 24:55

ۚ ۚ

The God has promised those among you who have believed and done righteous deeds that He will surely make them successors in the land, as He made those before them successors; establish for them their religion, which He has approved for them; and replace their fear with security. They worship Me, associating nothing with Me. Whoever disbelieves afterward—those are the defiantly disobedient.
