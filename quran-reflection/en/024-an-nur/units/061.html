An-Nur 24:61

ۚ ۚ ۚ

There is no restriction upon the blind, the lame, the sick, or yourselves in eating from your own houses, your fathers' houses, your mothers' houses, your brothers' houses, your sisters' houses, your paternal uncles' houses, your paternal aunts' houses, your maternal uncles' houses, your maternal aunts' houses, those whose keys you possess, or your friend's. There is no blame upon you in eating together or separately. When you enter houses, greet one another with a greeting from The God, blessed and good. Thus The God makes the signs clear to you, so that you may reason.
