An-Nur 24:21

ۚ ۚ ۗ

O you who have believed, do not follow the footsteps of Satan. Whoever follows the footsteps of Satan—he commands indecency and wrongdoing. Were it not for the favor of The God upon you and His mercy, none of you would ever have become pure. But The God purifies whom He wills. The God is hearing, knowing.
