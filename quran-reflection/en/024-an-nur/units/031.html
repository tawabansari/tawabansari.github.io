An-Nur 24:31

ۖ ۖ ۖ ۚ

Tell the believing women to lower their gaze, guard their private parts, and not display their adornment except what is apparent of it. Let them draw their coverings over their neckline openings. Let them not display their adornment except to their husbands, their fathers, their husbands' fathers, their sons, their husbands' sons, their brothers, their brothers' sons, their sisters' sons, their women, those whom their right hands possess, male attendants without desire, or children who have not become aware of women's private parts. Let them not stamp their feet so that their hidden adornment becomes known. Turn to The God together, O believers, so that you may prosper.
