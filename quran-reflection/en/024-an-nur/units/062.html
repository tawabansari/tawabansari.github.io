An-Nur 24:62

ۚ ۚ ۚ

The believers are only those who believe in The God and His messenger and, when with him on a collective matter, do not leave until they ask his permission. Those who ask your permission are the ones who believe in The God and His messenger. When they ask your permission for some affair of theirs, grant permission to whom you will among them and ask The God to forgive them. The God is forgiving, merciful.
