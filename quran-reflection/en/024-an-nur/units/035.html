An-Nur 24:35

ۚ ۖ ۖ ۚ ۗ ۚ ۗ

The God is the light of the heavens and the earth. The likeness of His light is a niche containing a lamp; the lamp is in glass, the glass like a brilliant star, lit from a blessed tree, an olive, neither eastern nor western. Its oil almost shines even though no fire has touched it. Light upon light! The God guides whom He wills to His light. The God presents examples for people, and The God knows all things.
