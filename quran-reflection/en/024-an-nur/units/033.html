An-Nur 24:33

ۗ ۖ ۚ ۚ

Let those who cannot find the means for marriage remain chaste until The God enriches them from His favor. Those whom your right hands possess who seek a contract—make a contract with them if you know good in them, and give them from the wealth of The God that He has given you. Do not compel your slave women to prostitution, if they desire chastity, seeking the goods of worldly life. Whoever compels them—The God, after their compulsion, is forgiving, merciful.
