Al-Fath 48:25

ۚ ۖ ۚ

They are the ones who disbelieved and barred you from the Sacred Mosque, detaining the sacrificial offering from reaching its place. Were it not for believing men and women whom you did not know, whom you might trample and thereby incur harm unknowingly—so that The God may admit into His mercy whom He wills. Had they separated, We would have punished those among them who disbelieved with a painful punishment.
