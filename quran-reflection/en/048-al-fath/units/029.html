Al-Fath 48:29

ۚ ۖ ۖ ۚ ۚ ۗ

Muhammad is the messenger of The God. Those with him are firm against the disbelievers, merciful among themselves. You see them bowing and prostrating, seeking bounty from The God and pleasure. Their mark is on their faces from the trace of prostration. That is their description in the Torah. Their description in the Gospel is like a crop that puts forth its shoot, strengthens it, grows thick, and stands upon its stalks, delighting the farmers, so that He may anger the disbelievers through them. The God has promised those among them who believe and do good deeds forgiveness and a great reward.
