Ash-Shura 42:15

ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ

So call to this, remain steadfast as you have been commanded, and do not follow their desires. Say, “I believe in whatever Book The God has sent down, and I have been commanded to act justly among you. The God is our Lord and your Lord. Our deeds are ours and your deeds are yours. There is no argument between us and you. The God will gather us together, and to Him is the destination.”
