Ash-Shura 42:13

ۖ ۚ ۚ

He prescribed for you of religion what He enjoined upon Noah, what We revealed to you, and what We enjoined upon Abraham, Moses, and Jesus: establish religion and do not become divided in it. What you call them to is burdensome to those who associate partners. The God chooses for Himself whom He wills and guides to Himself whoever turns back.
