Al-Imran 3:34

ۗ

Descendants, some of them from others; and The God is Hearing and Knowing.

Our Reflection

In Aal Imran 3:33, The God spoke of choosing Adam, Noah, the family of Abraham, and the family of Imran. Now the expression “ذُرِّيَّةً بَعضُها مِن بَعضٍ,” meaning “descendants, some of them from others,” points to the continuity of generations.

The word “ذُرِّيَّةً” refers to offspring and the continuation of generations, and can encompass both near and distant generations. The expression “بَعضُها مِن بَعضٍ” also expresses this connection, without explaining the precise relationship of every individual and family named.

The verse ends with “وَاللَّهُ سَميعٌ عَليمٌ,” meaning “and The God is Hearing and Knowing.” He hears His servants’ words and knows their circumstances and intentions.

These two attributes have a beautiful connection with the next verse. In Aal Imran 3:35, when Imran’s wife vows her child, she addresses The God with the same two attributes: “إِنَّكَ أَنتَ السَّميعُ العَليمُ,” meaning “surely You are the Hearing, the Knowing.” The ending of the present verse thus naturally prepares the way for the words and prayer of Imran’s wife.

Cross-references
  • Aal Imran 3:33 — Adam, Noah, the family of Abraham, and the family of Imran as those divinely chosen.
  • Aal Imran 3:35 — The vow of Imran’s wife and her repetition of the attributes Hearing and Knowing.
  • Aal Imran 3:38 — Zechariah’s request for good offspring and his addressing The God as the Hearer of prayer.
