Al-Imran 3:5

Indeed, nothing on earth or in heaven remains hidden from The God.

Our Reflection

This verse recalls God’s encompassing knowledge: nothing on earth or in heaven, whether apparent or hidden, lies outside His awareness.

In Al-Baqarah 2:77, we examined how God knows what people conceal and disclose. Al-Baqarah 2:284 also made clear that even a person’s hidden intentions and decisions remain within His knowledge and accounting.

This verse has particular significance following the warning in Aal Imran 3:4. Someone who knowingly denies or distorts truth may conceal their intention from people, but not from God. Likewise, a person’s hidden honesty and goodness do not go unnoticed by Him.

Ibrahim 14:38 expresses this truth in nearly identical words. Al-Baqarah 2:255 likewise recalls that God’s knowledge encompasses all, whereas created beings have access only to what He wills of His knowledge.

Cross-references
  • Al-Baqarah 2:77: God knows what people conceal and disclose.
  • Al-Baqarah 2:255: God’s encompassing knowledge and the limits of created beings’ knowledge.
  • Al-Baqarah 2:284: Divine knowledge and accounting for what is hidden within a person.
  • Aal Imran 3:4: The warning concerning denial of divine signs.
  • Aal Imran 3:29: God’s knowledge of what is concealed or disclosed within the breasts.
  • Al-Anam 6:59: God’s knowledge of the unseen and all beings, even a falling leaf.
  • Ibrahim 14:38: Nothing in heaven or on earth is hidden from God.
  • Saba 34:3: Not even a particle’s weight in the heavens or earth is hidden.
  • Ghafir 40:16: On the day people emerge, nothing about them is hidden from God.
  • At-Taghabun 64:4: God’s knowledge of what is in the heavens, the earth, and human hearts.
