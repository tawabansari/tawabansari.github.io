Al-Imran 3:38

ۖ ۖ

There Zechariah called upon his Lord. He said: My Lord, grant me from Yourself good offspring; surely You are the Hearer of prayer.

Our Reflection

The word “هُنالِكَ” connects Zechariah’s prayer with the event in the preceding verse. As Mary’s guardian, Zechariah had seen her good upbringing and the provision he found with her, and had heard her words about The God’s providing without reckoning. Seeing Mary stirred such longing in his heart that, despite his old age and his wife’s barrenness, he asked his Lord for a child—circumstances also described in Aal Imran 3:40 and Maryam 19:4.

He says: “رَبِّ هَب لى مِن لَدُنكَ ذُرِّيَّةً طَيِّبَةً,” meaning “My Lord, grant me from Yourself good offspring.”

“هَبْ,” from the root W H B, means giving and bestowing. Zechariah does not merely wish to have a child; he asks for a pure and worthy child. Just as he had seen Mary’s good upbringing, he now wishes to receive such a blessing himself.

He ends his prayer with “إِنَّكَ سَميعُ الدُّعاءِ,” meaning “Surely You are the Hearer of prayer.” This expression recalls the prayer of Mary’s mother in Aal Imran 3:35, where she addressed The God as “السَّميعُ العَليمُ,” the Hearing, the Knowing. With the same trust, Zechariah presents his request directly to his Lord. The answer to this prayer is stated in the next verse.

Cross-references
  • Aal Imran 3:35 — Mary’s mother’s prayer and her addressing The God as the Hearing, the Knowing.
  • Aal Imran 3:37 — Mary’s good upbringing and the observation of divine provision, setting the context for Zechariah’s prayer.
  • Aal Imran 3:39 — The good news of John’s birth in response to Zechariah’s prayer.
  • Aal Imran 3:40 — Zechariah’s old age and his wife’s barrenness.
  • Maryam 19:4 — Zechariah’s words about weakened bones and white hair in old age.
  • Maryam 19:5 — Zechariah’s request for a successor despite his wife’s barrenness.
  • Maryam 19:6 — Zechariah’s request that the child be pleasing to the Lord.
