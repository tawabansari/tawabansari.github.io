Al-Imran 3:47

ۖ ۚ

She said: My Lord, how shall I have a child when no man has had intercourse with me? He said: Thus it is; The God creates whatever He wills. Whenever He decrees a matter, He only says to it: Be, and it is.

Our Reflection

In the reflection on the preceding verse, we discussed Jesus’ particular distinction, his connection with Spirit of The Holy, and the emergence of awareness within him from the very beginning of his human life. We now consider another dimension of this exceptional creation.

As we observed when comparing the accounts of Zechariah and Mary in Aal Imran, the response to Zechariah’s wonder speaks of The God doing what He wills, whereas the response to Mary’s wonder speaks of His creating what He wills. The principle “Be, and it is” then explains how Jesus is created.

Jesus: the manifestation of The God’s word in the material world

To understand the significance of “Be, and it is” in Jesus’ story, we return to the verse announcing glad tidings to Mary:

إِذْ قَالَتِ الْمَلَائِكَةُ يَا مَرْيَمُ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يُبَشِّرُكِ بِكَلِمَةٍ مِنْهُ اسْمُهُ الْمَسِيحُ عِيسَى ابْنُ مَرْيَمَ وَجِيهًا فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ وَمِنَ الْمُقَرَّبِينَ

Aal Imran 3:45

When the angels said: O Mary, indeed The God gives you glad tidings of a word from Him whose name is the Messiah, Jesus son of Mary, honored in this world and the Hereafter and among those brought near.

The Quran does not say that Mary was given glad tidings of a human being who would be created by a word; rather, she was given glad tidings of a word from The God whose name is the Messiah, Jesus son of Mary.

Even in the Arabic structure, despite kalimah being grammatically feminine, the masculine pronoun in “اسْمُهُ” follows it, because that word refers to Jesus himself.

This truth is expressed more explicitly in Surah An-Nisa:

يَا أَهْلَ الْكِتَابِ لَا تَغْلُوا فِي دِينِكُمْ وَلَا تَقُولُوا عَلَى اللَّهِ إِلَّا الْحَقَّ ۚ إِنَّمَا الْمَسِيحُ عِيسَى ابْنُ مَرْيَمَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ وَكَلِمَتُهُ أَلْقَاهَا إِلَىٰ مَرْيَمَ وَرُوحٌ مِنْهُ ۖ فَآمِنُوا بِاللَّهِ وَرُسُلِهِ ۖ وَلَا تَقُولُوا ثَلَاثَةٌ ۚ انْتَهُوا خَيْرًا لَكُمْ ۚ إِنَّمَا اللَّهُ إِلَٰهٌ وَاحِدٌ ۖ سُبْحَانَهُ أَنْ يَكُونَ لَهُ وَلَدٌ ۘ لَهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ ۗ وَكَفَىٰ بِاللَّهِ وَكِيلًا

An-Nisa 4:171

O People of the Book, do not go to excess in your religion, and say nothing about The God except the truth. The Messiah, Jesus son of Mary, is only The God’s Messenger and His word which He conveyed to Mary, and a spirit from Him. So believe in The God and His Messengers, and do not say “Three”; desist—it is better for you. The God is only one deity; exalted is He above having a child. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on earth, and The God suffices as the One entrusted with all affairs.

To understand “word” in these verses, we must recognize that in the Quran it is not limited to utterance and speech. A judgment, promise, or command realized in the material world is also called a word.

For example:

وَأَوْرَثْنَا الْقَوْمَ الَّذِينَ كَانُوا يُسْتَضْعَفُونَ مَشَارِقَ الْأَرْضِ وَمَغَارِبَهَا الَّتِي بَارَكْنَا فِيهَا ۖ وَتَمَّتْ كَلِمَتُ رَبِّكَ الْحُسْنَىٰ عَلَىٰ بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ بِمَا صَبَرُوا ۖ وَدَمَّرْنَا مَا كَانَ يَصْنَعُ فِرْعَوْنُ وَقَوْمُهُ وَمَا كَانُوا يَعْرِشُونَ

Al-Araf 7:137

And We made the people who had been oppressed inherit the eastern and western parts of the land We had blessed. Your Lord’s good word was fulfilled for the Children of Israel because of their patience, and We destroyed what Pharaoh and his people had been building and raising.

In this verse, the liberation of the Children of Israel from Pharaoh’s domination and their inheritance of the land constitute the fulfillment of the Lord’s word in the material world and in history.

Surah Ya-Sin also clearly expresses the relationship between The God’s command and “Be, and it is”:

إِنَّمَا أَمْرُهُ إِذَا أَرَادَ شَيْئًا أَنْ يَقُولَ لَهُ كُنْ فَيَكُونُ

Ya-Sin 36:82

His command, when He wills something, is only to say to it: Be, and it is.

If we now place Jesus’ identification as The God’s word in Aal Imran 3:45 and An-Nisa 4:171 alongside the explanation of his creation in the present verse, then, in our view, Jesus himself is a manifestation of “Be, and it is” and the realization of that word in the material world.

Although all creation lies within the scope of The God’s command, among the Prophets the Quran specifically describes Jesus as “a word from Him” and “His word”.

Be, and it is: the certainty of the command and the stages of its realization

In the reflection on Al-Baqarah 2:117, we explained that “Be, and it is” does not necessarily mean a being appears instantaneously in the material world. The expression signifies the efficacy and certainty of The God’s command being realized. The manner and stages of its realization also fall within that command.

Jesus’ creation is a clear example of this truth.

In Surah Maryam, the Spirit responds to Mary’s wonder:

قَالَ كَذَٰلِكِ قَالَ رَبُّكِ هُوَ عَلَيَّ هَيِّنٌ ۖ وَلِنَجْعَلَهُ آيَةً لِلنَّاسِ وَرَحْمَةً مِنَّا ۚ وَكَانَ أَمْرًا مَقْضِيًّا

Maryam 19:21

He said: Thus it is; your Lord has said: This is easy for Me; and so that We may make him a sign for people and a mercy from Us. It was a matter already decided.

فَحَمَلَتْهُ فَانْتَبَذَتْ بِهِ مَكَانًا قَصِيًّا

Maryam 19:22

So she conceived him and withdrew with him to a distant place.

فَأَجَاءَهَا الْمَخَاضُ إِلَىٰ جِذْعِ النَّخْلَةِ قَالَتْ يَا لَيْتَنِي مِتُّ قَبْلَ هَٰذَا وَكُنْتُ نَسْيًا مَنْسِيًّا

Maryam 19:23

Then the pains of childbirth drove her to the trunk of a palm tree. She said: If only I had died before this and been completely forgotten.

What Aal Imran expresses as “إِذَا قَضَىٰ أَمْرًا” is called “أَمْرًا مَقْضِيًّا” in Surah Maryam.

The God’s command had been decided, yet Jesus passed through life in his mother’s womb and birth. The Quran speaks of Mary’s pregnancy and labor pains without specifying the duration of her pregnancy.

The certainty of the divine command and its realization in stages in the material world do not conflict. Passing through stages of creation is itself part of the realization of “Be, and it is”.

Manifestations of the creative command in Jesus’ life

What is extraordinary in Jesus’ life is not only how he was created. In our view, manifestations of the creative power of the very command that Jesus embodied can also be observed within the limits of his human existence.

Consider the wording in the present verse and the verse two places later:

وَرَسُولًا إِلَىٰ بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ أَنِّي قَدْ جِئْتُكُمْ بِآيَةٍ مِنْ رَبِّكُمْ ۖ أَنِّي أَخْلُقُ لَكُمْ مِنَ الطِّينِ كَهَيْئَةِ الطَّيْرِ فَأَنْفُخُ فِيهِ فَيَكُونُ طَيْرًا بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ ۖ وَأُبْرِئُ الْأَكْمَهَ وَالْأَبْرَصَ وَأُحْيِي الْمَوْتَىٰ بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ ۖ وَأُنَبِّئُكُمْ بِمَا تَأْكُلُونَ وَمَا تَدَّخِرُونَ فِي بُيُوتِكُمْ ۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَآيَةً لَكُمْ إِنْ كُنْتُمْ مُؤْمِنِينَ

Aal Imran 3:49

And a Messenger to the Children of Israel: I have come to you with a sign from your Lord. I fashion for you from clay something in the shape of a bird, then breathe into it, and it becomes a bird by The God’s permission. I heal the congenitally blind and the person afflicted with leprosy, and bring the dead to life by The God’s permission. I inform you of what you eat and what you store in your houses. Indeed, in these are a sign for you, if you are believers.

The principle of divine creation in Aal Imran 3:47Jesus’ action in Aal Imran 3:49
يَخْلُقُ مَا يَشَاءُأَخْلُقُ لَكُمْ
يَقُولُ لَهُ كُنْفَأَنْفُخُ فِيهِ
فَيَكُونُفَيَكُونُ طَيْرًا بِإِذْنِ اللَّهِ

The shared root kh l q and the repetition of “فَيَكُونُ” in these two verses are noteworthy. The first speaks of The God’s command in creation; the second speaks of Jesus shaping clay, breathing into it, and life coming into being by The God’s permission.

We also read in the preceding reflection that, in Jesus’ creation, The God speaks of breathing His Spirit into Mary. Now Jesus himself speaks of breathing into a form he had fashioned from clay in the shape of a bird.

We will examine this similarity further in the reflection on Aal Imran 3:49.

The relationship between giving life and the command “Be, and it is” is also clearly stated in Surah Ghafir:

هُوَ الَّذِي يُحْيِي وَيُمِيتُ ۖ فَإِذَا قَضَىٰ أَمْرًا فَإِنَّمَا يَقُولُ لَهُ كُنْ فَيَكُونُ

Ghafir 40:68

He is the One who gives life and causes death; so whenever He decrees a matter, He only says to it: Be, and it is.

All Messengers depended on The God’s permission to bring a sign, as stated in Ar-Rad 13:38 and Ghafir 40:78. Yet what draws our attention in Jesus’ life is a collection of qualities beginning with how he came into being and continuing throughout his human life.

These include speaking in the cradle, healing congenital blindness and leprosy beyond ordinary human medical capabilities at that time, bringing forth a bird from clay, raising the dead, and knowing things people did in their homes.

The God recalls a collection of these favors in Surah Al-Maidah:

إِذْ قَالَ اللَّهُ يَا عِيسَى ابْنَ مَرْيَمَ اذْكُرْ نِعْمَتِي عَلَيْكَ وَعَلَىٰ وَالِدَتِكَ ۖ إِذْ أَيَّدْتُكَ بِرُوحِ الْقُدُسِ تُكَلِّمُ النَّاسَ فِي الْمَهْدِ وَكَهْلًا ۖ وَإِذْ عَلَّمْتُكَ الْكِتَابَ وَالْحِكْمَةَ وَالتَّوْرَاةَ وَالْإِنْجِيلَ ۖ وَإِذْ تَخْلُقُ مِنَ الطِّينِ كَهَيْئَةِ الطَّيْرِ بِإِذْنِي فَتَنْفُخُ فِيهَا فَتَكُونُ طَيْرًا بِإِذْنِي ۖ وَتُبْرِئُ الْأَكْمَهَ وَالْأَبْرَصَ بِإِذْنِي ۖ وَإِذْ تُخْرِجُ الْمَوْتَىٰ بِإِذْنِي ۖ وَإِذْ كَفَفْتُ بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ عَنْكَ إِذْ جِئْتَهُمْ بِالْبَيِّنَاتِ فَقَالَ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا مِنْهُمْ إِنْ هَٰذَا إِلَّا سِحْرٌ مُبِينٌ

Al-Maidah 5:110

When The God said: O Jesus son of Mary, remember My favor upon you and your mother: when I supported you with Spirit of The Holy, so that you spoke to people in the cradle and in middle age; when I taught you the Book, wisdom, the Torah, and the Gospel; when, by My permission, you fashioned from clay something in the shape of a bird, then breathed into it, and it became a bird by My permission; when you healed the congenitally blind and the person afflicted with leprosy by My permission; when you brought forth the dead by My permission; and when I restrained the Children of Israel from you, when you came to them with clear proofs and those among them who denied said: This is nothing but obvious magic.

This verse places Jesus’ support through Spirit of The Holy alongside his exceptional abilities and emphasizes The God’s permission four times.

In Aal Imran 3:49, the verbs of creating, breathing, healing, and giving life are spoken directly by Jesus himself. These acts were realized within his human existence and by The God’s permission.

As we examined in Gabriel and the Spirit in the Quran and Divine Permission: The Order of Creation, Guidance, and Human Choice, The God’s permission relates to the possibilities and conditions under which phenomena occur within creation.

Is there a connection between Jesus being a word, his special relationship with Spirit of The Holy, and the emergence of these exceptional abilities in his human existence? Does what the Quran describes about Jesus’ creation and life reveal further dimensions of the creative power of The God’s command?

The God’s creative power and the denial of divine parenthood

Jesus’ extraordinary creation and exceptional abilities do not remove him from the position of a servant. The first words the Quran reports from Jesus in the cradle state this truth:

قَالَ إِنِّي عَبْدُ اللَّهِ آتَانِيَ الْكِتَابَ وَجَعَلَنِي نَبِيًّا

Maryam 19:30

He said: I am the servant of The God; He has given me the Book and made me a Prophet.

He is a manifestation of The God’s command, not a manifestation of The God’s essence.

Consider the striking similarity between the structure of Mary’s question in the present verse and the Quran’s argument against The God having a child:

بَدِيعُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ ۖ أَنَّىٰ يَكُونُ لَهُ وَلَدٌ وَلَمْ تَكُنْ لَهُ صَاحِبَةٌ ۖ وَخَلَقَ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ ۖ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عَلِيمٌ

Al-Anam 6:101

He is the Originator of the heavens and the earth. How could He have a child when He has had no consort, and He created everything and knows everything?

Mary’s question in Aal Imran 3:47The Quran’s argument in Al-Anam 6:101
أَنَّىٰ يَكُونُ لِي وَلَدٌأَنَّىٰ يَكُونُ لَهُ وَلَدٌ
وَلَمْ يَمْسَسْنِي بَشَرٌوَلَمْ تَكُنْ لَهُ صَاحِبَةٌ
اللَّهُ يَخْلُقُ مَا يَشَاءُوَخَلَقَ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ

In both verses, The God’s creative power is the key to understanding the matter.

Mary is one of The God’s creatures, living within the reproductive order, and wonders how she can have a child without contact with a man. The God, however, is Himself the Creator of that order; His creative power is not limited by the laws He established for His creatures’ reproduction.

In Jesus’ own story, the Quran connects this truth with the denial of The God having a child, His transcendence, and the principle “Be, and it is”:

مَا كَانَ لِلَّهِ أَنْ يَتَّخِذَ مِنْ وَلَدٍ ۖ سُبْحَانَهُ ۚ إِذَا قَضَىٰ أَمْرًا فَإِنَّمَا يَقُولُ لَهُ كُنْ فَيَكُونُ

Maryam 19:35

It is not fitting for The God to take a child; exalted is He. Whenever He decrees a matter, He only says to it: Be, and it is.

We observed this same connection in Al-Baqarah 2:116 and Al-Baqarah 2:117.

Attributing fatherhood to The God arises from projecting His creatures’ family relationships onto their Creator. Yet according to the principle of divine transcendence, The God is beyond such human conceptions.

Jesus’ creation is a sign of The God’s power, not evidence that He has a child. Jesus is The God’s word, His Messenger, a spirit from Him, and His servant.

In Aal Imran 3:59, the Quran illuminates this truth from another angle by comparing Jesus’ creation with Adam’s. We will discuss this in the reflection on that verse.
