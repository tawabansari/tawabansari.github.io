Al-Imran 3:40

ۖ

He said: My Lord, how will I have a boy when old age has reached me and my wife is barren? He said: Thus it is; The God does whatever He wills.

Our Reflection

Sometimes a person longs for something without being able to imagine how it could come about. In Aal Imran 3:37, we read that Zechariah found provision with Mary whose source amazed him, and Mary told him that The God provides for whomever He wills without reckoning. Seeing Mary, Zechariah wished for a child like her and, in Aal Imran 3:38, asked his Lord for good offspring, although he was elderly and his wife was barren. Now that he has received the good news that his prayer has been answered, he seeks assurance about how such a thing is possible. The God’s response is clear: The God does whatever He wills.

John’s birth prepares the way for understanding the miracle of Jesus’ birth. If someone accepts that The God can bring forth a child from an elderly man and a barren woman, how can they regard Jesus’ birth without a father as impossible? The similarity between Zechariah’s question here and Mary’s question in Aal Imran 3:47, and The God’s response to both, reveals this connection. Furthermore, in Aal Imran 3:39, even before John’s birth, The God describes him as confirming “a word from The God”—a word identified in Aal Imran 3:45 as the Messiah, Jesus son of Mary.
