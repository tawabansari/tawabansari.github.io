Al-Imran 3:7

ۖ ۗ ۗ ۗ

He is the One who sent down the Book upon you. In it are firm verses, the foundation of the Book, and others ambiguous. Those with deviation in their hearts follow what is ambiguous, seeking trial and seeking its interpretation. None knows its interpretation except The God. Those firmly grounded in knowledge say: We believe in it; all is from our Lord. None remember except people of understanding.
