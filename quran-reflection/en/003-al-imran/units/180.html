Al-Imran 3:180

ۖ ۖ ۗ ۗ

Let those who withhold what The God has given them from His bounty not think it is good for them. Rather, it is bad for them. What they withheld will be hung around their necks on the Day of Resurrection. To The God belongs the inheritance of the heavens and the earth, and The God is aware of what you do.
