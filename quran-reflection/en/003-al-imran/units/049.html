Al-Imran 3:49

ۖ ۖ ۖ ۚ

And as a messenger to the Children of Israel: “I have brought you a sign from your Lord. I form for you out of clay the shape of a bird, then breathe into it, and it becomes a bird by The God’s permission. I heal the one born blind and the leper, and bring the dead to life by The God’s permission. And I inform you of what you eat and what you store in your houses. In this is a sign for you, if you are believers.
