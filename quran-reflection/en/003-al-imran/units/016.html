Al-Imran 3:16

Those who say: Our Lord, we have indeed believed; so forgive our sins and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.

Our Reflection

This verse directly continues Aal Imran 3:15, describing those to whom The God has promised gardens of Paradise and His approval. They are the ones who practice taqwa, and the Qur’an now introduces their words and prayer.

“رَبَّنَا إِنَّنَا آمَنَّا فَاغْفِرْ لَنَا ذُنُوبَنَا”

“Our Lord, we have indeed believed; so forgive our sins.”

Despite their faith and taqwa, these people do not consider themselves sinless or without need of forgiveness. They have believed in their Lord, yet they know that a human being may commit errors and sins and always needs divine forgiveness. In Aal Imran 3:135, The God also speaks of those who seek His forgiveness after wrongdoing and do not knowingly persist in their sin.

“وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ”

“And protect us from the punishment of the Fire.”

The word “وَقِنَا” comes from the root و ق ي, meaning to preserve and protect. The same root occurs in “اتَّقَوْا” in the preceding verse. This connection in meaning matters: taqwa is a person’s conscious care over their own conduct, while “وَقِنَا” is a request for protection and help from the Lord. Thus, alongside their responsibility to avoid sin, human beings also need divine forgiveness and mercy.

The prayer is also expressed with plural pronouns: “رَبَّنَا,” “لَنَا,” “ذُنُوبَنَا,” and “وَقِنَا.” They do not ask for forgiveness and deliverance only for themselves individually; they include others in their prayer as well.

This verse shows that faith and taqwa do not mean claiming to be sinless or immune to error. Those who seek the approval of The God both accept responsibility for their conduct and humbly ask their Lord for forgiveness and deliverance from punishment.

Cross-references
  • Aal Imran 3:15: The promise of gardens of Paradise and divine approval to the people of taqwa, whose prayer this verse presents.
  • Aal Imran 3:17: Further qualities of the same group.
  • Al-Baqarah 2:201: Asking for good in this world and the Hereafter, with the same prayer: “وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ”.
  • Aal Imran 3:135: Remembering The God and seeking forgiveness after sin, without knowingly persisting in it.
  • Aal Imran 3:193: A similar prayer declaring faith and asking for sins to be forgiven.
