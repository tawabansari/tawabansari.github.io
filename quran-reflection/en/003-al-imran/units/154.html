Al-Imran 3:154

ۖ ۖ ۗ ۗ ۖ ۗ ۖ ۗ

Then, after the grief, He sent down upon you security: a drowsiness that covered a group of you. Another group cared only about themselves, thinking of The God what was untrue, the thought of ignorance. They said, “Do we have any part in the matter?” Say, “The matter belongs entirely to The God.” They conceal within themselves what they do not reveal to you. They say, “If we had any part in the matter, we would not have been killed here.” Say, “Even if you had been in your houses, those for whom killing was decreed would have gone out to their resting places.” And so that The God might test what is in your breasts and purify what is in your hearts. The God knows what is within the breasts.
