Al-Imran 3:195

ۖ ۖ ۗ

Their Lord answered them: “I do not let the work of any worker among you, male or female, be lost. You are of one another. Those who emigrated, were driven from their homes, were harmed in My path, fought, and were killed—I will remove their misdeeds and admit them to gardens beneath which rivers flow, a reward from The God.” With The God is the finest reward.
