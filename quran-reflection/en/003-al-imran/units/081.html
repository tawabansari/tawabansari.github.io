Al-Imran 3:81

ۚ ۖ ۚ

When The God took the covenant of the prophets: “Whatever I give you of a Book and wisdom, then a messenger comes to you confirming what is with you, you must believe in him and support him.” He said, “Do you affirm and accept My binding pledge on that?” They said, “We affirm.” He said, “Then bear witness, and I am with you among the witnesses.”
