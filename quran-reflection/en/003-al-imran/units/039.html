Al-Imran 3:39

Then the angels called to him while he was standing in the sanctuary, establishing salat: The God gives you good news of John, confirming a word from The God, noble and self-restrained, and a Prophet among the righteous.

Our Reflection

In Aal Imran 3:38, despite his old age and his wife’s barrenness, Zechariah asked his Lord for good offspring. Now, while he stands in the sanctuary establishing salat, the angels bring him the good news of John’s birth.

“وَهُوَ قائِمٌ يُصَلّى فِى المِحرابِ,” meaning “while he was standing in the sanctuary, establishing salat.”

The word “قَائِمٌ” comes from Q W M, a root connected with standing, rising, being upright, and firmness. In this verse, Zechariah performs salat while standing. In other verses, the Quran speaks of “establishing salat,” meaning setting up and maintaining this worship.

This verse also shows that salat existed before Muhammad. In Ibrahim 14:40, Abraham asks his Lord to make him and his descendants establishers of salat. We have examined the difference between “reciting prayers” and “establishing salat,” and how to establish it, in the article “Salat in the Quran”.

The angels describe John through four qualities: “مُصَدِّقًا بِكَلِمَةٍ مِنَ اللَّهِ,” confirming a word from The God; “سَيِّدًا,” noble and holding a position of leadership; “حَصُورًا,” self-restrained and in control of his desires; and “نَبِيًّا مِنَ الصّٰلِحينَ,” a Prophet among the righteous.

“Ḥaṣūr” speaks of self-restraint and control of desires; this description does not establish that John had a physical or sexual incapacity.

Another important point is John’s being described as confirming “a word from The God.” In Aal Imran 3:45, the Quran speaks of a word whose name is “the Messiah, Jesus son of Mary.”

Aal Imran 3:45

“إِذْ قَالَتِ الْمَلَائِكَةُ يَا مَرْيَمُ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يُبَشِّرُكِ بِكَلِمَةٍ مِنْهُ اسْمُهُ الْمَسِيحُ عِيسَى ابْنُ مَرْيَمَ وَجِيهًا فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ وَمِنَ الْمُقَرَّبِينَ”

“When the angels said: Mary, surely The God gives you good news of a word from Him whose name is the Messiah, Jesus son of Mary, honored in this world and the Hereafter, and among those brought near.”

John is thus introduced as confirming a word from The God that the Quran later identifies as the Messiah, Jesus son of Mary. This connection matters for understanding both figures in the following verses.

Cross-references
  • Aal Imran 3:38 — Zechariah’s prayer for good offspring, setting the context for the announcement of John’s birth.
  • Aal Imran 3:40 — Zechariah’s old age and his wife’s barrenness, which made John’s birth astonishing to him.
  • Aal Imran 3:45 — A word from The God identified as the Messiah, Jesus son of Mary.
  • Al-Anam 6:85 — Zechariah, John, Jesus, and Elijah among the righteous, directly connected with “among the righteous.”
  • Ibrahim 14:40 — Abraham’s request that he and his descendants establish salat.
  • Maryam 19:7 — The announcement of John’s birth and his naming.
  • Maryam 19:12 — The command to John to hold firmly to the Book, and the gift of judgment in childhood.
  • Al-Anbiya 21:73 — Establishing salat among the divine commands to leaders before Muhammad.
  • Al-Ankabut 29:45 — Establishing salat connected with remembrance of The God and restraint from indecency and wrongdoing.
