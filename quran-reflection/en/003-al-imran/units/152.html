Al-Imran 3:152

ۖ ۚ ۚ ۖ ۗ

The God fulfilled His promise to you when you were killing them by His permission, until you lost heart, disputed over the command, and disobeyed after He had shown you what you loved. Among you are those who desire this world, and among you are those who desire the Hereafter. Then He turned you away from them to test you. He has pardoned you, and The God is bountiful toward the believers.
