Al-Imran 3:75

ۗ

Among the People of the Book is one who, if you entrust him with a great sum, will return it to you; and among them is one who, if you entrust him with a dinar, will not return it to you unless you remain standing over him. That is because they say, “We bear no liability concerning the unlettered.” And they tell lies about The God while they know.
