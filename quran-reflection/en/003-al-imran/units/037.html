Al-Imran 3:37

ۖ ۖ ۖ ۖ

Her Lord accepted her with a good acceptance, caused her to grow well, and placed her in Zechariah’s care. Whenever Zechariah entered the sanctuary to visit her, he found provision with her. He said: Mary, where is this from? She said: It is from The God. Indeed, The God provides for whom He wills without reckoning.
