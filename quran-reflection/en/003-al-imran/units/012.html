Al-Imran 3:12

ۚ

Say to those who disbelieve: You will be defeated and gathered to Hell; what an evil resting place.

Our Reflection

Following the account of Pharaoh and those around him, this verse warns those who knowingly deny truth that their apparent power and superiority will not endure.

“قُلْ لِلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا سَتُغْلَبُونَ”

“Say to those who disbelieve: You will be defeated.”

“Satughlabun,” from GH L B, means being overcome and defeated. The defeat of arrogant systems is not confined to the hereafter. In Al-Qasas 28:40, the Quran states that God seized Pharaoh and his troops and drowned them in this world. Al-Anfal 8:54 likewise presents the destruction of Pharaoh’s people as a consequence of their denial of the signs and their wrongdoing.

Thus Pharaoh, despite all his power and resources, could not prevent defeat or escape the consequences of his conduct.

“وَتُحْشَرُونَ إِلَىٰ جَهَنَّمَ وَبِئْسَ الْمِهَادُ”

“And you will be gathered to Hell; what an evil resting place.”

“Tuhsharun,” from H SH R, refers to gathering, while “al-mihad” means a bed or resting place, used here to condemn Hell as a destination.

The verse therefore speaks of two consequences: defeat in this world and accountability in the hereafter. Pharaoh’s story shows that power and arrogance neither guarantee lasting victory nor save a person from the consequences of denying truth.

History also offers many examples of the defeat and dependence of governments that declared their opponents disbelievers in the name of religion. Governments associated with the Wahhabi school are one such example. The First Saudi State, which declared its religious opponents disbelievers, was destroyed in 1818 by Ottoman-Egyptian forces. Under the 1915 treaty, the government of Abdulaziz Al Saud also faced restrictions on its independence in foreign relations and British colonial influence, until Britain recognized the full independence of his dominions in 1927. Today’s Saudi government, despite its formal independence, also relies on American support and military cooperation in defense and security. This is only one of many historical examples showing that declaring others disbelievers is neither evidence of being right, nor a guarantee of victory, nor a barrier against defeat and dependence on other powers.
