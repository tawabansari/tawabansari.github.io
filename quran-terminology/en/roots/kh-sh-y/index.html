Qur'anic Root

Qur'anic Root KH SH Y

Reverent awe; an aware, humbling fear before greatness and truth

Short Note

This page is a placeholder so verse links to the root KH SH Y have a destination. The full study of this root will be written later.

In Al-Baqarah 2:74, reverent awe is not blind fear; it is a form of recognition that has an effect, softens the heart, and lowers arrogance.
