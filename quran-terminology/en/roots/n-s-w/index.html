Qur'anic Root N S W

Women, groups of women, and the female part of society.

In 2:49, نساءكم refers to your women. In the Pharaonic policy, they were kept alive, but this keeping alive was not mercy; it was part of controlling and restraining a dominated society.

This root is kept separate from ن س أ. The root ن س أ is more closely tied to delay, postponement, granting time, and nasi'.
