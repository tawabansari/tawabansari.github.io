Qur'anic Root

Qur'anic Root Q L L

Being little, becoming few, being slight, or being small before a greater matter

Short Study

The root ق ل ل is tied to smallness, fewness, and slightness. In the Qur'an, this may refer to quantity or to the poverty of value before something greater.

In Al-Baqarah 2:41, قَلِيلًۭا reminds us that every price is small before the signs of God, even if it appears to bring influence, temporary security, power, or social gain.
