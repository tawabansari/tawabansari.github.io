Qur’anic Vocabulary

Qur’anic Roots Index

Many Qur’anic words are built from shared roots. Studying a root helps the reader see how related words carry connected meanings across different verses.

This section gathers Qur’anic roots into individual study pages. Each root page explores the root’s general meaning, important Qur’anic forms, transliteration, related word families, and how the root shapes the meaning of the verses where it appears.

The aim is not to detach words from the Qur’an. The aim is to help the reader return to the verses with clearer attention, so that a word is understood not only by a quick translation, but also by its wider Qur’anic family.

This index is arranged by Arabic root order. As more root studies are completed, they will be added here.

A

  • ا ل ه A L H Worship, turning toward, placing something in the station of a god, and recognizing The One God

Ḥ

  • ح م د Ḥ M D Conscious praise, recognition of goodness and excellence, gratitude joined with acknowledgment, and the disclosure of worthiness for ḥamd

R

  • ر ب ب R B B Lordship, nurturing, ownership joined with governance, bringing a being toward growth, and the right of command based on rubūbiyyah
  • ر ح م R Ḥ M Mercy, nurturing care, protective nearness, forgiveness, and the opening of a path toward growth and return
