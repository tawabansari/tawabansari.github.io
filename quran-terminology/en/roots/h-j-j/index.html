Qur'anic Root

Qur'anic Root H J J

Intending, presenting proof, and arguing with evidence before another

Short Note

This page is a placeholder so verse links to the root H J J have a destination. The full study of this root will be written later.

In Al-Baqarah 2:76, “yuhajjukun” points to the fear that an opened truth will become proof and argument against those who conceal it.
