Qur’anic Root S B Q

Preceding, getting ahead

فَٱسْتَبِقُوا۟

Plural imperative of istabaqa, Form VIII; in 2:148, compete with one another in good deeds. The field of competition here is doing good.

This brief note explains the word in 2:148. A detailed root study has not yet been added.
