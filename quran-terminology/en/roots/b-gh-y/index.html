Qur'anic Root

Qur'anic Root B GH Y

Seeking, desiring, transgressing in pursuit, rivalry, and crossing proper limits

Short Note

This page is a placeholder so verse links to the root B GH Y have a destination. The full study of this root will be written later.

In the reflection on Al-Baqarah 2:75, this root is mentioned to explain deviation after knowledge, when knowledge becomes joined with superiority-seeking, envy, rivalry, monopolizing, and the desire to preserve power.
