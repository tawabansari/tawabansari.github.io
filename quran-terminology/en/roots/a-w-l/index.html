Qur'anic Root

Qur'anic Root A W L

Beginning, precedence, being first, and returning a matter to its origin or first point

Short Study

The root أ و ل is connected with being first, taking precedence, beginning, and at times returning something to its origin or first point.

In Al-Baqarah 2:41, أَوَّلَ كَافِرٍۭ بِهِۦ warns the People of the Book not to stand in the first rank of covering the confirming revelation, especially because their prior Book and history of revelation give them greater responsibility to recognize truth.
