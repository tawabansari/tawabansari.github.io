Qur’anic Root

The Qur’anic Root W S M

Marking, placing a sign, identifying, and making something recognizable

Brief Explanation

The root W S M is connected with marking, placing a sign, and making something identifiable. From this angle, an ism is something that brings a thing out of ambiguity and makes it recognizable.

This analysis helps explain ٱلْأَسْمَآء (al-asma), because a name is not merely a verbal label. It can be an identifier that discloses the reality, features, and function of a thing for human understanding and interaction.
