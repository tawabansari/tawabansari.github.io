Core meaning: length and extension in size or time; in another branch, possession of ability, resources, bounty, or power.

1. The root in early Arabic

The triliteral root ط و ل occupies two main semantic fields: length and extension, as opposed to shortness; and possession of ability, resources, bounty, and ample means.

In the first field, طَالَ describes something extended in size or duration: tall stature, a long road, a lengthy night, or time that seems prolonged.

In the second, طَوْل denotes ability and resources enabling someone to act, meet a need, or give to another. Physical size and duration are no longer the subject; capacity and means are.

A witness from pre-Islamic Arab life

The Muʿallaqah attributed to the pre-Islamic poet Ṭarafah ibn al-ʿAbd contains:

لَعَمْرُكَ إِنَّ الْمَوْتَ مَا أَخْطَأَ الْفَتَى

la-ʿamruka inna al-mawta mā akhṭaʾa al-fatā

لَكَالطِّوَلِ الْمُرْخَى وَثِنْيَاهُ بِالْيَدِ

la-ka-l-ṭiwali al-murkhā wa-thinyāhu bi-l-yad

Translation: By your life, death, while it misses the young man, is like a long tether let loose, with its two ends still held in the hand.

الطِّوَل names a long rope used to tether a grazing animal, allowing movement within its length. The owner could let out more rope for a wider grazing area while retaining the ability to draw it in.

The poet uses this image for human destiny: a person may live for many years, yet death awaits, just as the animal's freedom within the tether does not release it from the rope.

The verse illustrates a material use of the family in early Arab life. The long tether was a concrete tool whose name relates directly to length and extension.

The attribution is preserved in familiar transmissions of Ṭarafah's Muʿallaqah, although the precise date of composition cannot be established independently of that attribution.

This witness supports the early length sense. طَوْل as means, power, and bounty requires separate consideration. Arabic dictionaries record these senses, and the Quran attests them in the language of revelation. The existence of the length sense alone does not establish when the means sense arose.

2. The word family and its principal meanings

Important forms include طَالَ, يَطُولُ, طُول, طَوْل, طَوِيل, أَطْوَل, طَوَّلَ, أَطَالَ, تَطَاوَلَ, اسْتَطَالَ, طَائِل, and طَائِلَة.

These forms occur in several semantic branches.

Length and extension

طَالَ الشَّيْءُ means that something became long or extended. With a physical object, it concerns dimensions, such as a person's height, a rope's length, or a road.

With time, the same root concerns duration: a prolonged night, lifespan, or wait.

طُول names this quality, in contrast to قِصَر. طَوِيل describes something long or prolonged.

Length does not imply greatness in every dimension. Something can be long yet small in width, volume, or quantity. طُول should therefore not always be treated as absolute magnitude.

Ability, ample means, and resources

طَوْل, with fatḥah on the first consonant and sukūn on wāw, is recorded for bounty, ability, ample means, and resources.

ذُو طَوْلٍ describes someone possessing the resources or capacity to act or give to others.

This branch relates semantically to abundance and possession. Just as tall stature can enable a person to reach something beyond a shorter person's reach, wealth, power, or resources can extend a person's capacity to act.

The analogy makes the semantic relationship intelligible; it is not conclusive evidence for how the means sense historically developed from length.

Lengthening and allowing more time

أَطَالَ and طَوَّلَ mean lengthening or extending something.

أَطَالَ الْكَلَامَ means prolonging speech. طَوَّلَ لَهُ can mean granting someone a respite or more time.

Here an agent causes a duration or dimension to increase.

تَطَاوَلَ: extension and seeking superiority

One use of تَطَاوَلَ is becoming extended or prolonged.

Another is seeking superiority, raising oneself above others, or acting haughtily.

Morphological form alone therefore does not determine an ethical meaning. تَطَاوَلَ can describe prolonged time without conveying arrogance or pretension.

طَائِل and طَائِلَة

طَائِل and طَائِلَة are also recorded for abundance, benefit, value, or a substantial gain.

لَا طَائِلَ مِنْهُ describes something yielding no worthwhile benefit or result.

This usage is closer to ability and resources than physical dimensions, since it concerns the result, benefit, or means obtained from something.

These forms do not occur in the Quran and should be distinguished from its actual derivatives.

3. Quranic occurrences

The root occurs ten times in five derived forms:

  • طَالَ and its inflected occurrences: three.
  • تَطَاوَلَ, form VI: one.
  • طَوْل, ability, means, or bounty: three.
  • طُول, length or height: one.
  • طَوِيل, an adjective: two.

The Quran employs both physical or temporal extension and the branch concerning means and ability.

A. طُول: physical height

وَلَا تَمْشِ فِي الْأَرْضِ مَرَحًا ۖ إِنَّكَ لَنْ تَخْرِقَ الْأَرْضَ وَلَنْ تَبْلُغَ الْجِبَالَ طُولًا

wa-lā tamshi fī al-arḍi maraḥan innaka lan takhriqa al-arḍa wa-lan tablugha al-jibāla ṭūlā

Al-Isrāʾ 17:37

And do not walk upon the earth exultantly. You will never split the earth, nor reach the mountains in height.

Al-Isrāʾ 17:37

طُولًا here means height or vertical extension.

The verse confronts a person walking with arrogance with the reality of their physical scale. However great they imagine themselves, such behaviour neither splits the earth nor raises their stature to mountain height.

طُولًا plainly concerns dimensions and height. The condemnation of arrogance comes from the verse as a whole and its relation to مَرَحًا, rather than from the root ط و ل itself.

A measurable physical property is thus used in the verse's ethical argument.

B. طَوِيل: temporal extension

إِنَّ لَكَ فِي النَّهَارِ سَبْحًا طَوِيلًا

inna laka fī al-nahāri sabḥan ṭawīlā

Al-Muzzammil 73:7

Indeed, during the day you have prolonged activity.

Al-Muzzammil 73:7

طَوِيلًا qualifies سَبْحًا and expresses its extended duration.

The passage concerns rising at night and attending to the Quran. Daytime is contrasted as a period of movement and extensive activity.

طويل means prolonged; the kind of movement or activity comes from سبح and the context.

The same adjective occurs elsewhere:

وَمِنَ اللَّيْلِ فَاسْجُدْ لَهُ وَسَبِّحْهُ لَيْلًا طَوِيلًا

wa-mina al-layli fa-sjud lahu wa-sabbiḥhu laylan ṭawīlā

Al-Insān 76:26

And during part of the night prostrate to Him, and glorify Him for a long portion of the night.

Al-Insān 76:26

طَوِيلًا qualifies لَيْلًا. Here the expression conveys an extended period of nighttime glorification, rather than necessarily limiting the instruction to nights that are seasonally long.

Both occurrences express extension. What differs is the thing qualified: daytime activity in one passage and nighttime duration in the other.

C. طَالَ: prolonged time, life, and waiting

طَالَ occurs in three verses concerning temporal extension.

In Ṭā Hā 20:86, Moses asks his people after returning whether the time of the promise had seemed long to them. أَفَطَالَ عَلَيْكُمُ الْعَهْدُ concerns the period of waiting or time since the promise.

Al-Anbiyāʾ 21:44 describes people and their fathers being allowed enjoyment until life became prolonged for them.

Both uses concern time, while their evaluations come from context. A long life can provide opportunity and enjoyment; prolonged waiting can involve testing, impatience, or forgetfulness.

The third occurrence is in Al-Ḥadīd:

أَلَمْ يَأْنِ لِلَّذِينَ آمَنُوا أَنْ تَخْشَعَ قُلُوبُهُمْ لِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ وَمَا نَزَلَ مِنَ الْحَقِّ وَلَا يَكُونُوا كَالَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْكِتَابَ مِنْ قَبْلُ فَطَالَ عَلَيْهِمُ الْأَمَدُ فَقَسَتْ قُلُوبُهُمْ ۖ وَكَثِيرٌ مِنْهُمْ فَاسِقُونَ

a-lam yaʾni li-lladhīna āmanū an takhshaʿa qulūbuhum li-dhikri allāhi wa-mā nazala mina al-ḥaqqi wa-lā yakūnū ka-lladhīna ūtū al-kitāba min qablu fa-ṭāla ʿalayhimu al-amadu fa-qasat qulūbuhum wa-kathīrun minhum fāsiqūn

Al-Ḥadīd 57:16

Has the time not come for those who believe that their hearts should humble themselves to the remembrance of The God and the truth that has come down, and that they should not be like those given the Book before, for whom time grew long and whose hearts hardened? Many of them are defiantly disobedient.

Al-Ḥadīd 57:16

فَطَالَ عَلَيْهِمُ الْأَمَدُ concerns a period becoming prolonged.

أَمَد is an independent word for a duration or temporal limit. طال expresses its lengthening.

The passage then mentions hardened hearts. That outcome belongs to the warning's context, not the lexical definition of طال.

Nor does the verse state that every long-lived person, or everyone who has known truth for a long time, inevitably becomes hard-hearted. The warning concerns time passing alongside estrangement from remembrance and hardening of the heart.

In all three occurrences, طال expresses temporal extension, while its consequences vary with context.

D. تَطَاوَلَ: extension over time

وَلَٰكِنَّا أَنْشَأْنَا قُرُونًا فَتَطَاوَلَ عَلَيْهِمُ الْعُمُرُ ۚ وَمَا كُنْتَ ثَاوِيًا فِي أَهْلِ مَدْيَنَ تَتْلُو عَلَيْهِمْ آيَاتِنَا وَلَٰكِنَّا كُنَّا مُرْسِلِينَ

wa-lākinnā anshaʾnā qurūnan fa-taṭāwala ʿalayhimu al-ʿumuru wa-mā kunta thāwiyan fī ahli madyana tatlū ʿalayhim āyātinā wa-lākinnā kunnā mursilīn

Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:45

But We brought forth generations, and time grew long for them. You were not dwelling among the people of Midian, reciting Our signs to them; but We were sending.

Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:45

فَتَطَاوَلَ is form VI and here expresses time becoming prolonged.

The context concerns generations arising and time passing between earlier events and later audiences.

تَطَاوَلَ عَلَيْهِمُ الْعُمُرُ should therefore not be restricted to unusually long individual lifespans. قُرُونًا makes the passage of time across generations relevant to understanding the phrase.

The meaning is close to طال, with a different verbal form expressing temporal extension.

This occurrence does not convey arrogance or pretension, although تَطَاوَلَ can carry those meanings elsewhere in Arabic.

4. طَوْل: means, power, and resources

Three further occurrences belong to طَوْل. This branch differs from physical and temporal length and deserves separate treatment.

Ability and resources needed to act

In Al-Nisāʾ 4:25, طَوْلًا concerns the means required for marriage.

The verse describes someone without the means to marry believing muḥṣanāt women, then sets out an alternative under stated conditions.

طَوْلًا here means neither tall stature, a lengthy duration, nor patience. It concerns the means available for entering that marriage.

Given the subject and its financial obligations, economic means are a clear instance. The lexical meaning should not, however, be reduced to a specified sum or marriage gift. The word has a broader range of ability and resources; context specifies the relevant capacity.

أُولُو الطَّوْل: those possessing means

وَإِذَا أُنْزِلَتْ سُورَةٌ أَنْ آمِنُوا بِاللَّهِ وَجَاهِدُوا مَعَ رَسُولِهِ اسْتَأْذَنَكَ أُولُو الطَّوْلِ مِنْهُمْ وَقَالُوا ذَرْنَا نَكُنْ مَعَ الْقَاعِدِينَ

wa-idhā unzilat sūratun an āminū bi-llāhi wa-jāhidū maʿa rasūlihi istaʾdhanaka ulū al-ṭawli minhum wa-qālū dharnā nakun maʿa al-qāʿidīn

Al-Tawbah 9:86

And when a surah is sent down: Believe in The God and strive alongside His Messenger, those among them possessing means ask your permission, saying: Leave us to remain with those who sit behind.

Al-Tawbah 9:86

أُولُو means possessors or holders. It has no singular of the same wording; ذُو is its semantic singular. الطَّوْل here denotes means and resources.

أُولُو الطَّوْلِ therefore identifies people possessing resources, especially those relevant to the responsibility described in the verse.

Rather than participate in striving alongside the Messenger, these people ask to remain among those sitting behind.

The verse distinguishes possessing ability from using it rightly. Resources alone do not demonstrate fulfilment of responsibility or moral virtue.

Lexically, طول concerns possessions and capacity here, not the owners' ethical superiority. Their conduct in context supplies the grounds for criticism.

ذِي الطَّوْل: a divine description

غَافِرِ الذَّنْبِ وَقَابِلِ التَّوْبِ شَدِيدِ الْعِقَابِ ذِي الطَّوْلِ ۖ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ۖ إِلَيْهِ الْمَصِيرُ

ghāfiri al-dhanbi wa-qābili al-tawbi shadīdi al-ʿiqābi dhī al-ṭawli lā ilāha illā huwa ilayhi al-maṣīr

Ghāfir 40:3

Forgiver of sin and Accepter of repentance, severe in punishment, Possessor of abundant bounty; there is no deity except Him, and to Him is the destination.

Ghāfir 40:3

ذِي الطَّوْلِ is a Quranic description of The God.

ذِي means possessor or holder; الطَّوْل belongs to the field of bounty, ability, and abundant means.

Context distinguishes this from أُولُو الطَّوْلِ in Al-Tawbah.

Al-Tawbah 9:86 describes humans possessing resources relative to others. Ghāfir 40:3 applies the expression to The God alongside forgiveness, acceptance of repentance, and severity in punishment.

Here ذِي الطَّوْلِ emphasizes divine bounty, whose scope is not limited to human possessions and circumstances.

“Possessor of abundant grace and bounty” is therefore an appropriate rendering.

Ability and power also belong to the semantic field. “Possessor of bounty and boundless power” may be used in explanation, but “boundless” is not supplied by the word alone in direct translation; it depends on a broader understanding of divine attributes.

طول here does not mean divine physical height. It belongs to bounty and capacity and should not be confused with طُول as a physical dimension.

The Quran uses ذِي الطَّوْلِ. When identifying the Quranic description, retain that expression rather than replacing it without justification with another title such as الطويل.

5. Related roots

Ṭ W L and F Ḍ L: means and abundance

فَضْل, from ف ض ل, concerns increase, superiority, or what exceeds a particular measure or share.

طَوْل overlaps with فضل in the means branch, but can especially emphasize resources and capacity enabling action.

Al-Nisāʾ 4:25 concerns means for marriage. In Ghāfir 40:3, the divine description emphasizes abundant bounty.

The roots are close in some contexts, but فضل cannot be replaced by طول indiscriminately.

Ṭ W L and Q D R: resources and ability

ق د ر, in its ability uses, concerns power or capacity to do something.

طَوْل can additionally convey possession of the necessary resources.

Someone may be physically capable of a task without having the necessary financial means. Ability and wealth are not identical in such a case.

In Al-Nisāʾ 4:25, لَمْ يَسْتَطِعْ with طَوْلًا identifies means relevant to marriage.

طول and power can nevertheless be close in some Arabic contexts; the distinction should not be absolute.

Ṭ W L and M D D: length and extending

م د د often concerns stretching, extending, or expanding.

طُول more directly names the length or extension of an object or period.

A lengthy night is not identical to an action that extends something. طويل describes a duration or dimension, whereas forms of م د د can emphasize the act of extension.

أَطَالَ and طَوَّلَ also mean lengthening, however. The difference between the families is therefore not uniform across all derivatives.

Ṭ W L and ʾ M D: length and a temporal span

أَمَد concerns a duration or temporal limit; طول describes that duration's length.

Al-Ḥadīd 57:16 combines them: a period grew long for a group of people.

أمد alone identifies a span or limit. طال adds its prolongation.

They are not synonyms: one identifies the span, the other describes its extension.

Ṭ W L and GH N Y: resources and sufficiency

غِنَى, from غ ن ي, concerns freedom from need and sufficiency.

طَوْل in the means branch concerns possessing resources or capacity to act or give.

Someone may have enough for their own needs without ample resources to help others. Conversely, someone with substantial resources may still feel dependent because of other needs and obligations.

Resources and freedom from need are therefore not identical, although غنى and طول can be close in Arabic usage.

6. Translation proposals for Forqan

Choose the translation according to the form and the subject of the verse.

طُول: length, height, extension. “Height” suits Al-Isrāʾ 17:37, which concerns mountains' elevation.

طَوِيل: long or prolonged. These are natural equivalents in Al-Muzzammil 73:7 and Al-Insān 76:26.

طَالَ: became long or was prolonged. In Ṭā Hā 20:86, Al-Anbiyāʾ 21:44, and Al-Ḥadīd 57:16, it concerns time.

تَطَاوَلَ: became extended or prolonged. In Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:45, “time grew long for them” fits.

طَوْل: ability, means, resources, bounty, or ample provision. In Al-Nisāʾ 4:25, “the necessary means”; in Al-Tawbah 9:86, “those possessing means”; in Ghāfir 40:3, “abundant grace and bounty.”

Brief root meaning: length and extension in size or time; in another branch, ability, resources, and bounty.

Temporal meaning: a duration, lifespan, or wait becoming prolonged.

Economic and social meaning: possessing the ability and means required for an action.

Meaning in ذِي الطَّوْلِ: Possessor of bounty, power, and abundant generosity.

7. Conclusion

ط و ل has two principal semantic fields: length and extension; and ability and means.

Length is apparent in dimensions, roads, ropes, and duration. The verse attributed to Ṭarafah ibn al-ʿAbd also shows the family in the name of a long animal tether, a familiar tool of early Arab life.

In the other branch, طَوْل concerns ability, bounty, ample means, and resources. The Quran applies this branch to humans and to The God, with the scope determined by context.

The Quran uses the root for mountain height, extended night and daytime activity, prolonged life and time, economic means, and divine bounty.

طُول as length and طَوْل as capacity and resources must remain distinct in translation. Duration itself carries no necessary positive or negative value, just as resources alone establish neither virtue nor corruption.

Final definition: the first branch of ط و ل concerns length or extension in size or time. The second concerns ability, resources, bounty, and ample means enabling action or giving. Abundance and extension make their semantic relationship intelligible, but neither branch's historical priority is certain without independent evidence.
