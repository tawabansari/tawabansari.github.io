Qur'anic Root

Qur'anic Root N H R

River, a visible and flowing current of water

Short Note

This page is a placeholder so verse links to the root N H R have a destination. The full study of this root will be written later.

In Al-Baqarah 2:74, “rivers” pictures abundant water flowing from what appears outwardly hard and closed.
