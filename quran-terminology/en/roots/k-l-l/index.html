Qur’anic Root

The Qur’anic Root K L L

Wholeness, totality, comprehensiveness, and including all parts

Brief Explanation

The root K L L is connected with wholeness, totality, and comprehensiveness: including all the parts or the whole scope intended by the context.

In expressions such as كُلَّهَا (kullaha), the meaning returns to everything intended in the verse’s context, not a limited or scattered part.
