Qur’anic Root

The Qur’anic Root Ṣ Ḥ B (Sad H B)

Companionship, accompaniment, association, close keeping, and enduring relation

Brief Explanation

The root Ṣ Ḥ B (Sad H B) is connected with companionship, accompaniment, close association, and having an enduring relation with someone or something.

In the expression أَصْحَـٰبُ ٱلنَّار (ashab al-nar), the meaning is not merely being located inside fire. It points to an existential companionship with the reality that arises from covering truth and denying the signs.
