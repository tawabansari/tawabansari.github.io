Qur’anic Root

The Qur’anic Root KH L F

Coming after, succeeding, following behind, and standing in continuation

Brief Explanation

The root KH L F is connected with coming after another, standing behind, succeeding, and continuing a path or order.

In the word خَلِيفَة (khalifah), the root points to a being placed on earth in a station of responsibility, agency, decision, and effect. A khalifah is not merely one who comes later; the word points to a role in which trust, reform, corruption, and response to divine command become visible.
