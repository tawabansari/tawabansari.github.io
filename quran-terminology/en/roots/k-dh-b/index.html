Qur’anic Root

The Qur’anic Root K DH B

Lying, declaring false, treating as untrue, and breaking the credibility of a report or sign

Brief Explanation

The root K DH B is connected with lying, falsehood, declaring something untrue, and attributing falsity.

In the Qur’an, denying the signs is not merely ordinary disagreement. It is a stance against signs that should awaken the human being toward truth.
