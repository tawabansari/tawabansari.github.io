Qur’anic Root

The Qur’anic Root N B A

Weighty news, significant report, and informing about a matter of consequence

Brief Explanation

The root N B A differs from ordinary news. A naba is a report that carries weight, significance, and cognitive force.

In Al-Baqarah 2:31, the command أَنۢبِـُٔونِى (anbi'uni) is not merely a request to repeat a sound. It asks for disclosure of knowledge concerning the names and identifiers of what has been presented.
