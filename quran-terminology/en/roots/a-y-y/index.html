Qur’anic Root

The Qur’anic Root A Y Y

Sign, clear marker, and something that points beyond itself to a greater truth

Brief Explanation

The word ayah is connected with a sign or clear marker: something that is not limited to itself, but points the human being toward a truth beyond it.

The signs of The God may be revelation, signs in the horizons and within the self, or clear divine evidences that place the human being before truth.
