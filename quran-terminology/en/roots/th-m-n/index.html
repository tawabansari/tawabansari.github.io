Qur'anic Root

Qur'anic Root TH M N

Price, value, and what is taken in exchange for something else

Short Study

The root ث م ن belongs to the field of price and exchange: what a person takes in return for something else.

In Al-Baqarah 2:41, ثَمَنًۭا points to a price taken in exchange for God's signs. This price is not only money. Any political, social, religious, financial, or reputational gain that replaces truth is, before the signs of God, a small price.
