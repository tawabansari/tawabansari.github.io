Qur’anic Root

The Qur’anic Root S B H

Moving smoothly, flowing in a course, and not drowning or leaving the path

Brief Explanation

The root S B Ḥ (S B H) is connected with swimming, moving smoothly, flowing in a course, and not slipping out of the right orbit.

In tasbih, a being moves within the appointed divine course and declares The One God free from deficiency, lack of wisdom, incapacity, or disorder. In this sense, tasbih stands opposite corruption: corruption is leaving soundness and orbit, while tasbih is remaining in the right current.
