Qur'anic Root AYN L N

Making public, disclosing, announcing, and making visible

Short Note

This page is a placeholder so verse links to the root AYN L N have a destination. The full study of this root will be written later.

In Al-Baqarah 2:77, “yu‘linun” stands opposite “yusirrun” and points to what a human being makes public, displays, or announces before people.
