Qur'anic Root GH F L

Heedlessness, unawareness, and lack of attention

Short Note

This page is a placeholder so verse links to the root GH F L have a destination. The full study of this root will be written later.

In Al-Baqarah 2:74, the end of the verse emphasizes that The God is not heedless of the hardness of the heart or of the action that comes out of it.
