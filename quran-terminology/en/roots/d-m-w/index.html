Qur’anic Root

The Qur’anic Root D M W

Blood, the fluid of bodily life, and a sign of embodied life

Brief Explanation

The root D M W is connected with blood and bodily life. Blood is a sign of embodied life, living flow, and the sanctity of the self.

In the expression سَفْكُ ٱلدِّمَآءِ (safk al-dima), blood is not merely a bodily substance. It represents life and human sanctity, which bloodshed violates.
