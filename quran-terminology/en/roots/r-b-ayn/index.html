1. Short Introduction to the Root

The root ر ب ع appears in the Qur'an primarily in the field of number and counting. Its most important forms relate to the number four, one-fourth, fourfold grouping, and the number forty.

Its Qur'anic uses include four months, four witnesses, four birds, four days, one-fourth of inheritance, forty nights, and forty years.

One should not extract from the number four itself a firm theory of "geometric stability of the world," "the necessary framework of civilization," or "static balance of structures." Four may be connected in some natural and human arrangements with directions, corners, or division, but the Qur'an does not state such a general law for this root.

This root must also not be mixed with ر ب و in the verse:

اهْتَزَّتْ وَرَبَتْ

It stirred and swelled.

Al-Hajj 22:5

رَبَتْ is from ر ب و and means rising or increasing, not from ر ب ع.

The shortest statement of this root is:

a numerical root indicating four, fourfoldness, one-fourth, and in another construction, forty.

4. The Semantic Core of the Root

The semantic core of ر ب ع in Qur'anic use is counting and division on the basis of four.

This root can indicate four units, a portion equal to one-fourth, the number forty, or a fourfold distribution.

From this numerical core alone, one cannot conclude that four is always a symbol of stability, earth, house, cardinal directions, or civilizational completion.

In the Qur'an, a number may simply determine quantity. For example:

أَرْبَعَةُ أَشْهُرٍ

Four months.

At-Tawbah 9:2

In such cases, adding meanings like "geometry of settlement" or "balance of four-sided forces" goes beyond the wording.

5. The Material and Counting Image of the Root

The basic image of this root is dividing a set into four parts or counting four units.

If something is divided into four equal parts, each part is رُبُع, one-fourth.

If four things stand together, أربعة or أربع is used to count them.

If four tens are considered, أربعون, forty, is formed.

This image is mathematical and counting-based. It need not become a theory about four-legged support, four seasons, or square construction.

A four-legged object may be an example of four support points, but not all stable structures have four supports. Three points can also form a stable plane, and increasing the number of supports does not always increase stability.

The suitable image is therefore: four units, four parts, one-fourth, or four tens.

6. Use of the Root in Early Arabic

In Arabic, أربعة and أربع are used for the number four.

الرُّبع is one-fourth of something.

أربعون is forty.

Other derivatives in Arabic can relate to staying, lodging, or entering spring pasture, but those branches must be studied separately with lexical evidence.

The existence of such uses does not mean that the core of every branch is "geometric stability."

Also, مربع is used in Arabic for a square, but common Arabic usage should not be confused with a Qur'anic derivative.

7. History of Understanding, Misuse, or Symbolic Expansion

Numbers in religious and cultural traditions sometimes acquire symbolic meaning. Four has been linked with four directions, four seasons, four elements, or four corners, and forty has often been connected with maturity, trial, or completion of a period.

Some of these connections may be meaningful in cultures, but they should not be turned without clear textual evidence into the meaning of the root or of all Qur'anic verses.

For example, Musa's forty nights are truly a defined period, but the Qur'an does not say that forty is the "hardware code of maturation for all systems."

Likewise, four witnesses in the legal ruling for accusation are not a "four-sided geometric balance of society"; they are a legal number for a particular level of proof.

The correct method is first to understand the number as a number, then examine separately whether the context gives additional meaning.

8. Methodological Note: Number, Symbol, and Meaning

In studying Qur'anic numbers, three levels must be distinguished.

First, the direct numerical meaning, such as four months or forty nights.

Second, the role of the number in a ruling or story, such as four witnesses or forty years of wandering.

Third, symbolic reflection, such as connecting four with directions or forty with maturity.

The third level must not replace the first two.

When the Qur'an mentions a number, one should first ask: what is being counted, whether the number is exact or approximate, whether it appears in a legal ruling or historical narrative, and whether the text itself gives the number symbolic meaning.

Without a clear answer, turning a number into a universal law of creation or civilization is not defensible.

9. A Short Look at Related Semitic Languages

Forms close to ر ب ع appear in Semitic languages for the number four, and this numerical family is very old.

This kinship shows that the concept of four was shared in ancient layers of Semitic languages.

But from usages such as "the four corners of the world" in ancient texts, one cannot conclude that the original meaning of the root was "the geometric boundary of settled life."

Such expressions may be cultural uses of the number four, not the definition of the root.

10. Qur'anic Uses of the Root

A. Four months of respite

فَسِيحُوا فِي الْأَرْضِ أَرْبَعَةَ أَشْهُرٍ

So move through the earth for four months.

At-Tawbah 9:2

The number four here determines a specific duration. The meaning comes from the context of covenant, declaration of disavowal, and time granted. The number four by itself does not present a theory of political stability.

B. Four months in ila'

لِلَّذِينَ يُؤْلُونَ مِنْ نِسَائِهِمْ تَرَبُّصُ أَرْبَعَةِ أَشْهُرٍ

For those who swear abstention from their women, there is a waiting of four months.

Al-Baqarah 2:226

Four months here is a time limit for clarifying decision and ending uncertainty. Its importance is legal and familial.

C. Four months and ten days

يَتَرَبَّصْنَ بِأَنْفُسِهِنَّ أَرْبَعَةَ أَشْهُرٍ وَعَشْرًا

They wait concerning themselves four months and ten days.

Al-Baqarah 2:234

The number four is part of the waiting period of widowed women. One should not build from it a theory of bodily fourfoldness or cosmic cycles unless clear independent evidence exists.

D. Four witnesses

فَاسْتَشْهِدُوا عَلَيْهِنَّ أَرْبَعَةً مِنْكُمْ

Then call four from among yourselves as witnesses against them.

An-Nisa 4:15

And:

ثُمَّ لَمْ يَأْتُوا بِأَرْبَعَةِ شُهَدَاءَ

Then they do not bring four witnesses.

An-Nur 24:4

The number four establishes a heavy level of proof for a highly dangerous accusation. Its function is to protect honor, prevent easy accusation, and close the path of rumor. It should not be described as "four geometric pillars of justice."

E. Four testimonies in li'an

فَشَهَادَةُ أَحَدِهِمْ أَرْبَعُ شَهَادَاتٍ بِاللَّهِ

Then the testimony of one of them is four testimonies by The God.

An-Nur 24:6

Four testimonies form a specific legal structure, while the fifth has a different role. Here too the number is understood within the ruling.

F. Four birds

فَخُذْ أَرْبَعَةً مِنَ الطَّيْرِ فَصُرْهُنَّ إِلَيْكَ

Take four birds and draw them toward yourself.

Al-Baqarah 2:260

The number four specifies the birds involved in the sign shown to Ibrahim. The Qur'an does not explain why exactly four birds were chosen, so any symbolic meaning must be offered carefully.

G. Four days

وَقَدَّرَ فِيهَا أَقْوَاتَهَا فِي أَرْبَعَةِ أَيَّامٍ

And He measured its provisions in four days.

Fussilat 41:10

This verse speaks about the measuring of the earth's provisions. The relation of these four days to the two days of earth's creation requires study of the structure of the verses, but the number four itself does not mean "the four-season balance of earth." The Qur'an does not mention four seasons here.

H. Four wives

فَانْكِحُوا مَا طَابَ لَكُمْ مِنَ النِّسَاءِ مَثْنَىٰ وَثُلَاثَ وَرُبَاعَ

Then marry what is agreeable to you of women, two by two, three by three, and four by four.

An-Nisa 4:3

رُباع is distributive and means in fours. The verse comes in the context of justice toward orphans and fear of injustice. Its continuation emphasizes the condition of justice and restricts to one wife if injustice is feared. Rubaa is therefore only a permitted number within a conditional structure, not a geometric law of family.

I. Musa's forty nights

وَوَاعَدْنَا مُوسَىٰ ثَلَاثِينَ لَيْلَةً وَأَتْمَمْنَاهَا بِعَشْرٍ فَتَمَّ مِيقَاتُ رَبِّهِ أَرْبَعِينَ لَيْلَةً

We appointed for Musa thirty nights and completed them with ten; so the appointed time of his Lord was completed as forty nights.

Al-Araf 7:142

The verse expresses forty as thirty plus ten and emphasizes completion of the appointed time. This period was Musa's time of meeting and receiving, but the text does not say that forty is a universal law of maturity for all humans or civilizations.

J. Forty years of wandering

فَإِنَّهَا مُحَرَّمَةٌ عَلَيْهِمْ أَرْبَعِينَ سَنَةً يَتِيهُونَ فِي الْأَرْضِ

It is forbidden to them for forty years; they will wander in the earth.

Al-Maidah 5:26

Forty years is the duration of a punishment and historical wandering. It may in practice be related to generational change, but the verse itself does not call it a complete cycle of civilizational maturity.

K. Reaching forty years of age

حَتَّىٰ إِذَا بَلَغَ أَشُدَّهُ وَبَلَغَ أَرْبَعِينَ سَنَةً

Until, when he reaches his full strength and reaches forty years.

Al-Ahqaf 46:15

In this verse, forty years is connected with a stage of growth and self-awareness. It is one of the clearest places where forty is linked with human maturity. Yet even here, one should not conclude that every natural, political, or civilizational process is completed in exactly forty units.

L. One-fourth of inheritance

وَلَكُمْ رُبُعُ مَا تَرَكْنَ

And for you is one-fourth of what they leave.

An-Nisa 4:12

رُبُع is one part of four equal parts. This is a direct mathematical use and one of the clearest witnesses for the counting core of the root.

11. Important Derivatives in the Qur'an

أَرْبَعَة / أَرْبَع

Both are the number four; the difference in form relates to the grammatical gender of the counted noun.

أَرْبَعِينَ

The number forty. It appears in several contexts: Musa's appointed time, Bani Isra'il's wandering, and the human reaching forty years. These should not all be interpreted by one theory.

رُبُع

One-fourth of a whole.

رُبَاع

Fourfold or in groups of four.

مُرَبَّع

It does not appear in the Qur'an and should not be presented as a Qur'anic derivative.

مَرَابِع

It does not appear in the Qur'an as residence or settlement and has no role in Qur'anic use of the root.

12. Grammatical and Structural Notes

Gender disagreement between number and counted noun

Numbers three to ten in Arabic commonly disagree in grammatical gender with the counted noun. This rule is not specific to the number four.

Examples include أربعة أشهر and أربع شهادات.

Therefore the gender pattern in the number four cannot be made into a reflection of "opposing forces of four directions." It is a broad grammatical rule that also includes numbers such as three, five, and six.

أَرْبَعَةَ أَشْهُرٍ

أربعة is the number and أشهر is the plural genitive counted noun.

أَرْبَعُ شَهَادَاتٍ

أربع is the number and شهادات is the counted noun.

أَرْبَعِينَ لَيْلَةً

أربعين is the accusative number and ليلة is the singular accusative specification.

رُبُعُ مَا تَرَكْنَ

رُبع is part of the inheritance-share construction, and ما تركن identifies what is being divided into one-fourth.

مَثْنَىٰ وَثُلَاثَ وَرُبَاعَ

These are distributive forms: two by two, three by three, and four by four.

13. R B AYN Compared with TH L TH

Four and three are both numbers, and one cannot derive fixed value judgments from the numbers themselves.

In geometry, three is sufficient for a triangle, and three non-collinear points determine a plane.

Four can form a square, four directions, or four parts.

But these geometric differences do not mean that three is inherently fragile and four inherently stable.

In the Qur'an too, every number must be understood in its own context.

14. R B AYN Compared with AYN SH R

أربعة is four units, and عشرة is ten units.

أربعون structurally points to four tens.

But forty does not mean only the mechanical sum of four packets of ten. In the sentence, forty is an independent number for specifying quantity or duration.

One should not call ten "the packet of completion" and four "the geometry of settlement" unless a verse indicates that.

15. Limited Note: Forty and Maturity

The number forty appears in several important verses, and one of them explicitly connects it with human growth:

وَبَلَغَ أَرْبَعِينَ سَنَةً

And he reached forty years.

Al-Ahqaf 46:15

Reaching forty years in this verse is a stage in which a person can reflect with a more mature view on blessing, parents, righteous action, and descendants.

Musa's forty-night appointed time was also a complete period for a great event.

From these uses one may understand that forty in some contexts accompanies completion of a period or arrival at an important stage.

But this accompaniment must not be made into a universal law. The Qur'an does not say that every human reaches perfection exactly at forty, every society changes in forty years, or every natural process is completed in forty units.

A careful statement is: In some verses, forty accompanies completion of a period, an appointed time, or a stage of maturity, but its lexical meaning remains the number forty.

16. Specialized Discussion: Is Four a Symbol of Stability?

Four appears in many human and natural structures: four cardinal directions, four corners of a shape, four seasons in some climate classifications, and four supports in some structures.

This presence can cause four to be treated in various cultures as a symbol of order, expanse, or stability.

But several cautions are necessary. Not all stable phenomena are fourfold. The triangle is among the most stable engineering structures, and many structures rest on three points. Not all regions of earth have four distinct seasons. Enclosed space need not have four walls; it may be circular, triangular, or many-sided.

Fourth, the Qur'an does not introduce the Ka'bah through the root ر ب ع and does not use the word square for it.

Therefore the Ka'bah or the Ancient House cannot be taken as Qur'anic evidence for the root ر ب ع.

If the geometric form of the Ka'bah is discussed, that belongs to architecture and the history of the building, not to the lexical use of this root in the verses.

The best conclusion is: The number four may in some structures connect with balanced division or four directions, but the Qur'an does not present it as a universal law of geometric stability in the world.

18. Conclusion

The root ر ب ع in the Qur'an is primarily a numerical root and indicates four, one-fourth, fourfold distribution, and forty.

أربعة and أربع are both the number four, and their difference relates to the grammatical gender of the counted noun.

رُبع is one-fourth of a whole.

رُباع expresses fourfold distribution.

أربعون is the number forty.

The Qur'an uses the number four in various rulings and narratives: four months, four witnesses, four testimonies, four birds, four days, and fourfold grouping in the marriage construction.

The number forty appears in Musa's appointed time, Bani Isra'il's wandering, and the human being reaching forty years.

In some contexts, forty accompanies the completion of a period or arrival at a stage of maturity, but these uses do not prove that forty is a "hardware law of the evolution of all systems."

Likewise, the number four is not presented in the Qur'an as a universal law of geometric stability. Its connection with four directions, four seasons, four legs, or square construction may be cultural or geometric reflection, but it must not be imposed on the root meaning.

مربع and مرابع are not central Qur'anic derivatives of this root.

The verse:

اهْتَزَّتْ وَرَبَتْ

It stirred and swelled.

Al-Hajj 22:5

is from ر ب و and has no relation to ر ب ع.

The rule of gender disagreement between number and counted noun is not specific to four, and no theory of balancing opposing forces can be extracted from it.

The shortest statement of this root's core is:

a numerical root for expressing four, dividing a whole into four parts, fourfold distribution, and the number forty; with legal, temporal, and narrative uses whose precise meanings are learned from the context of each verse.
