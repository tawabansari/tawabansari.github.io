Qur'anic Root H D TH

Coming into being, becoming new, reporting, and speaking

Short Note

This page is a placeholder so verse links to the root H D TH have a destination. The full study of this root will be written later.

In Al-Baqarah 2:76, the form “atuhaddithunahum” points to informing, reporting, and allowing truth to move out of a closed circle into public speech.
