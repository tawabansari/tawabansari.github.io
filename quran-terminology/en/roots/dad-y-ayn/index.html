Qur’anic Root Ḍ Y ʿ

Being lost or wasted; causing loss or waste

لِيُضِيعَ

To let go to waste; in the negative construction of 2:143, God does not let your faith go to waste.

This brief note explains the word in 2:143. A detailed root study has not yet been added.
