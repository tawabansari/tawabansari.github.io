Qur'anic Root SH D D

Intensity, greater hardness, firmness, and becoming stronger

Short Note

This page is a placeholder so verse links to the root SH D D have a destination. The full study of this root will be written later.

In Al-Baqarah 2:74, “harder” shows that the human heart can become even more intense than stone in its closedness and hardness.
