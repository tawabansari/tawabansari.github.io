Qur'anic Root

Qur'anic Root F T H

Opening, unlocking, disclosing a path or truth, and opening judgment or decision

Short Note

This page is a placeholder so verse links to the root F T H have a destination. The full study of this root will be written later.

In Al-Baqarah 2:76, “fataha” points to what The God has opened: a truth or knowledge that must not become the private property of a group.
