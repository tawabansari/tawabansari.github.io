Qur'anic Root

Qur'anic Root W F Y

Completing, fulfilling, and not falling short in what is due

Short Study

The root و ف ي is tied to fully rendering what is due, completing a measure, and not falling short in covenant, promise, responsibility, or appointed measure.

In Al-Baqarah 2:40, أَوْفُوا۟ means: do not keep the divine covenant incomplete, selective, or dependent on interest; fulfill it completely and truthfully. In the same verse, أُوفِ points to God's fulfillment of His promise and appointment.
