Qur'anic Root

Qur'anic Root Q S W

Hardening, dryness, lack of softness, and becoming resistant to penetration

Short Note

This page is a placeholder so verse links to the root Q S W have a destination. The full study of this root will be written later.

In Al-Baqarah 2:74, this root describes the hardening of the heart before truth: a state in which a sign or reminder reaches the heart but does not soften it or make it receptive.
