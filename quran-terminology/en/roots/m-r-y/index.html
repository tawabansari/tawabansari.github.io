Qur’anic Root M R Y

Doubt and disputation over a matter

ٱلْمُمْتَرِينَ

Plural active participle of imtarā, Form VIII. In the reflection on 2:147, it describes those who draw a clear matter into wavering and dispute. This reading does not reject sincere inquiry or the possibility of reconsideration.

This brief note explains the word in 2:147. A detailed root study has not yet been added.
