Qur’anic Root

The Qur’anic Root S F K

Pouring out, shedding, and causing liquid to flow

Brief Explanation

The root S F K is connected with pouring out or causing something, especially a liquid, to flow.

When this root is joined with blood, as in يَسْفِكُ ٱلدِّمَآءَ (yasfiku al-dima), it is not merely a physical act. It pictures the violation of life’s sanctity, the violent pouring out of embodied life, and the destruction of human safety.
