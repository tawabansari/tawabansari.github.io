Qur’anic Root

The Qur’anic Root K W N

Being, becoming, existing in a state, and the emergence of a condition

Brief Explanation

The root K W N is connected with being, becoming, existing in a condition, and the appearance of a state.

In constructions such as إِن كُنتُمْ (in kuntum), this root points to the claimed or actual state of the addressees before a condition or test.
