Qur’anic Root SH Ṭ R

Direction, side; part, half

شَطْرَ / شَطْرَهُۥ

In 2:144, this word means toward or in the direction of the Sacred Mosque. In shaṭrahu, the attached pronoun refers to the Sacred Mosque.

This brief note explains the word in 2:144. A detailed root study has not yet been added.
