Qur'anic Root

Qur'anic Root DH K R

Remembering, bringing truth into presence, and preserving a sign in awareness, speech, and action

Short Study

The root ذ ك ر is not limited to mental recollection. Dhikr means that a truth which must not be forgotten becomes present again in awareness, speech, decision, and action.

In Al-Baqarah 2:40, ٱذْكُرُوا۟ calls the Children of Israel not merely to keep divine favor as historical memory, but to turn it into faithfulness, responsibility, and return to covenant.
