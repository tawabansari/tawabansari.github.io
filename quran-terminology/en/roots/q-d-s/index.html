Qur’anic Root

The Qur’anic Root Q D S

Purity, purifying, setting apart what is sacred, and preserving sanctity

Brief Explanation

The root Q D S is connected with purity, purification, sanctification, and preserving the boundary of what is pure and exalted.

In وَنُقَدِّسُ لَكَ (wa-nuqaddisu laka), taqdis means preserving the purity, boundary, and exaltedness of The One God against pollution, violation, and the breaking of the boundaries of life and right.
