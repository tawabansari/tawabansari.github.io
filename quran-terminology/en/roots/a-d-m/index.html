Qur’anic Root

The Qur’anic Root A D M

Adam, the first human, and in some analyses a link with earth, soil, or earth-colored clay

Brief Explanation

The name Adam is connected in lexical discussions with the first human and the beginning of the human story in the Qur’an. Some analyses also connect it with earth, soil, or the color of earth.

In Al-Baqarah 2:31, Adam represents the human being who receives the capacity for knowledge of the names, recognition of signs, and the unfolding of khalifah on earth.
