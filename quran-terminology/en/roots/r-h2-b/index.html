Qur'anic Root

Qur'anic Root R H B

Awe, vigilant reverence, careful restraint before greatness and consequence

Short Study

The root ر ه ب is not the field of blind fear. It is tied to awe, vigilance, restraint, and a weighty awareness before greatness and consequence.

In Al-Baqarah 2:40, فَٱرْهَبُونِ means that the center of rahbah must be God, not people, social power, religious rank, scholars, monks, or political pressure.
