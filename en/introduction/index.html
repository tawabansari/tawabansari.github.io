Introduction to Qur’an

This website seeks to approach the Qur’an free from the authority of secondary, sectarian, inherited, or political religious systems.

This website does not belong to any political party, any school of jurisprudence, or any organized movement.

We are Hanif — non-affiliated and independent seekers turned toward the Creator of the heavens and the earth. 6:79

We believe in The God, the Last Day, the angels, the Books, and the prophets. 2:177

We make no distinction between any of His messengers. 2:136

Whoever believes in The God and the Last Day and does righteous deeds — whether Jewish, Christian, Sabiʾin, or any other Hanif seeker — are our brothers and sisters in faith. 2:62

We are an Interfaith Society, and we strive to surpass one another only in goodness. 2:148

The most honored of us before God is the most mindful of Him. 49:13

Pure and exalted is The God above what they associate with Him, or attribute to Him, or describe about Him. 23:91 37:159
