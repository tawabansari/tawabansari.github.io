About Forqan

Independent Quran research, translation and reflection

Forqan is an educational project for studying and understanding the Quran, without affiliation to any sect, group, political party or religious denomination. We seek peace and coexistence under the banner of The God, alongside people of every faith and all humanity, with respect and a sense of responsibility toward His other creatures. Our principles and identity are set out in our Introduction to the Quran.

Along this path, we offer verse-by-verse translation and reflection, Quranic Arabic roots, concepts, and thematic articles in Persian and English.

Why this project exists

Our purpose is to seek the pleasure of The God by bringing our thoughts and actions into harmony with His mercy for all worlds. We begin with humanity and hope, through His mercy, that even if we are but a speck of goodness, we may, God willing, extend that goodness throughout existence.

How we work

Along this path, we strive to bring our thoughts and actions into harmony with the word of The God, the Noble Quran. In studying the Quran, we examine its language and word roots, the context of each passage, and the connections between verses, while distinguishing the Quranic text from translation and our own reflections.

Our reflections are human understandings, open to questions, examination and correction. Our methodology page explains our approach to translation and reflection, and its limitations.

Constructive criticism and public discussion

Tabsira is Forqan’s companion discussion space. Readers can question a specific verse interpretation or article, offer textual evidence, and propose alternative readings. Agreement with Forqan is not a condition of participation. Public discussion gives readers a place to examine objections and responses alongside the original work.

Read public discussions without an account, or sign in to contribute under the participation principles. Discussion is an opportunity for review and learning; it is not a claim of formal academic peer review. A comment does not automatically change the original text.

Visit TabsiraParticipation principles

Responsibility and corrections

Responsibility for Forqan’s published material rests with its author. Technical and AI tools do not replace that responsibility or establish the correctness of an interpretation. Readers are encouraged to identify the passage in question, explain their concern, and provide supporting evidence so that a correction can be considered.

Contact the team

For private questions or concerns, contact the team through Tabsira. For public criticism of a passage, use its discussion so other readers can follow the exchange.

Our Prayer

This is our prayer, inspired by the Quran revealed to Muhammad, the messenger of The God, and by the hanif tradition of Abraham, the devoted friend of The God, and his righteous way of faith:

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

In the name of The God, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful.

اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبُّنَا الَّذِي فَطَرَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ بِالْحَقِّ.

O God, You are our Lord, who brought the heavens and the earth into being in truth.

الْقَدِيمُ وَالْقَيُّومُ، لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِكَ شَيْءٌ، وَأَنْتَ السَّمِيعُ الْبَصِيرُ.

You are The Ancient, The Self-Subsisting and The Sustainer of all. Nothing is like You, and You are The All-Hearing, The All-Seeing.

اللَّهُمَّ أَسْلَمْنَا وُجُوهَنَا لَكَ، وَعَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْنَا، وَنَرْجُو رَحْمَتَكَ.

O God, we have submitted ourselves to You, placed our trust in You, and hope for Your mercy.

رَبَّنَا أَنْتَ الْوَاحِدُ الْقَهَّارُ، الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيمُ، وَلَمْ يَكُنْ لَكَ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ.

Our Lord, You are The One, The Supreme over all, The Most Exalted, The Magnificent. No one has ever been Your equal.

الْحَيُّ الَّذِي لَا يَمُوتُ، وَالْبَاقِي الَّذِي كُلُّ مَنْ عَلَيْهَا فَانٍ، وَيَبْقَىٰ وَجْهُ رَبِّنَا ذُو الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ.

You are The Ever-Living, who never dies, The Ever-Abiding. All who dwell upon the earth pass away, while the Face of our Lord, The Possessor of The Majesty and The Honor, remains.

آمَنَّا بِاللَّهِ وَمَلَائِكَتِهِ وَكُتُبِهِ وَرُسُلِهِ.

We believe in The God, His angels, His books, and His messengers.

لَا نُفَرِّقُ بَيْنَ أَحَدٍ مِنْ رُسُلِهِ.

We make no distinction between any of His messengers.

سَمِعْنَا وَأَطَعْنَا، غُفْرَانَكَ رَبَّنَا وَإِلَيْكَ الْمَصِيرُ.

We hear and obey. We seek Your forgiveness, our Lord, and to You is the return.

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْعِصْيَانِ.

O God, we seek refuge in You from disobedience.

وَنَعُوذُ بِكَ مِمَّا يُبَاعِدُنَا عَنْكَ.

And we seek refuge in You from whatever draws us away from You.

نَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ.

We seek refuge in You from Satan, the outcast.

وَنَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الطَّاغُوتِ وَالطُّغْيَانِ.

And we seek refuge in You from taghut and transgression.

اللَّهُمَّ نَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ أَنْفُسِنَا، وَنَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا تَعْلَمُ وَمِنْ شَرِّ مَا لَا نَعْلَمُ.

O God, we seek refuge in You from the evil within ourselves, from the evil of what You know, and from the evil of what we do not know.

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الْقَوِيِّ الْمُدَبِّرِ.

In the name of The God, the All-Strong, who directs all affairs.

رَبُّنَا الَّذِي أَعْطَىٰ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ خَلْقَهُ ثُمَّ هَدَىٰ.

Our Lord is the One who gave everything its creation, then guided it.

وَرَبُّنَا الَّذِي عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ.

And our Lord is the One who has power over all things.

وَأَمْرُهُ إِذَا أَرَادَ شَيْئًا أَنْ يَقُولَ لَهُ كُنْ فَيَكُونُ.

When He wills a thing, His command is to say to it, “Be,” and it comes to be.

رَبَّنَا وَسِعْتَ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ رَحْمَةً وَعِلْمًا

Our Lord, You encompass all things in mercy and knowledge.

اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْنَا مِنْ رَحْمَتِكَ الَّتِي وَسِعْتَ بِهَا الْعَالَمِينَ.

O God, make us part of Your mercy with which You embrace all worlds.

وَاجْعَلْنَا بِهَا مِنْ عِبَادِ اللَّهِ الصَّالِحِينَ.

And through it, make us among the righteous servants of The God.

وَاهْدِنَا بِهَا إِلَى الصِّرَاطِ الْمُسْتَقِيمِ.

And through it, guide us to the straight path.

اللَّهُمَّ إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ.

O God, You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help.
