English Articles

Long-form essays on the Qur’an, tawhid, language, religious history, economics, family, and the structures that pull people away from the clear criterion of The God’s signs.

This page is for pieces that need room to breathe. Some are essays, some are research notes, and some are closer to small books. The common thread is method: read the question through the Qur’an, then test inherited claims through language, reason, justice, and the real consequences of human life.

What Belongs Here?

Essays and long studies that open a subject carefully, from language and context to moral and social consequences.

What Is the Method?

The Qur’an remains the center, but questions are not detached from history, economics, family, language, or lived human reality.

How Should These Be Read?

Some entries are short; others are book-length. The longer pages are organized with internal chapter lists for slow reading.
